Pyromancer Class Guide — Conquest of Azeroth | July 2026 
 
 
 Pyromancer Overview
 Pyromancers deliver raw fire power through the Ignition mechanic. Incineration focuses on devastating burst and DoTs, Draconic transforms you into a mobile dragon-casting assassin, and Flameweaving introduces a rare fire-based healer path. Pyromancers shine in dungeons and PvP burst windows.
 Master of flame who channels destructive fire magic — or phoenix healing — to incinerate foes or mend allies.
 Class Mechanic — Ignition
 Pyromancers build heat through spellcasting, unlocking empowered fire abilities at higher ignition levels.
 Pyromancer uses Mana as its primary resource and wears Cloth armor.
 Specializations
 At level 10, Pyromancer players choose one of three specializations, each offering a distinct playstyle:
 Incineration: Burn enemies to cinders with powerful direct damage spells, meteors, and fire DoTs like Ignite and Blaze.
 Draconic: Embrace the Black Dragonflight as a mobile magical assassin with draconic breaths and a red dragon ascension form.
 Flameweaving: DPS-healer hybrid leaving trails of flame that mend allies and damage enemies, summoning a Phoenix pet.
 Strengths and Weaknesses
 Understanding where this class excels helps you decide if it fits your preferred content and playstyle.
 Strength: High burst damage
 Strength: Strong AoE potential
 Strength: Flameweaving offers unique healing path
 Weakness: Cloth armor vulnerability
 Weakness: Mana management in long fights
 Weakness: Draconic requires close-range positioning
 Leveling Tips
 Incineration simplifies early leveling with natural AoE. Keep Ignition stacked during pulls. Flameweaving becomes valuable once you join dungeon groups.
