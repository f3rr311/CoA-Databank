 Witch Doctor Overview
 Witch Doctors wield voodoo hex magics and primal Loa power. Brewing creates potions on the fly while healing through wards, Voodoo cripples enemies with stacked hexes, and Shadowhunting channels Vol'jin's legacy as a shadow-poisoned bow hunter with dinosaur companions.
 Troll voodoo master brewing potions, hexing foes, and summoning dinosaur spirits empowered by wild gods.
 Class Mechanic — Loa Spirits
 Witch Doctors commune with Loa wild gods, summoning primal dinosaurs and channeling voodoo hex magic.
 Witch Doctor uses Mana as its primary resource and wears Leather armor.
 Specializations
 At level 10, Witch Doctor players choose one of three specializations, each offering a distinct playstyle:
 Brewing: Mix potions mid-combat for situational buffs, channel voodoo beams, and place Healing Wards for ally sustain.
 Voodoo: Hex and jinx specialist stacking debuffs and DoTs to send foes to the Odda' Side as shallow husks.
 Shadowhunting: Vol'jin-inspired ranged hunter with shadow-poisoned arrows, invoked dinosaurs, and Serpent Wards.
 Strengths and Weaknesses
 Understanding where this class excels helps you decide if it fits your preferred content and playstyle.
 Strength: Brewing unique healer with potions
 Strength: Voodoo excellent crowd control
 Strength: Shadowhunting strong solo ranged
 Weakness: Potion mixing complexity in Brewing
 Weakness: Voodoo ramp-up on DoTs
 Weakness: Leather fragility
 Leveling Tips
 Shadowhunting handles solo content with ranged poison damage. Voodoo trivializes elites through hex transformation. Brewing becomes essential in dungeon groups.
