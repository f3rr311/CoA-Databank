 Tinker Overview
 Tinkers bring mechanical ingenuity to CoA with round-swapping gunplay and deployable gadgets. Demolition transforms battlefields with explosives, Mechanics builds turrets and mechs, and Invention heals through beacons and nanobots. The Rounds mechanic rewards preparation and tactical ammunition choices.
 Eccentric inventor deploying bombs, turrets, mechs, and an arsenal of bullet types on the battlefield.
 Class Mechanic — Rounds
 Tinkers carry an arsenal of unique round types for their gun, swapping ammunition for different effects.
 Tinker uses Rounds as its primary resource and wears Leather / Mail armor.
 Specializations
 At level 10, Tinker players choose one of three specializations, each offering a distinct playstyle:
 Demolition: Explosive specialist wielding blackpowder — rocket launchers, handcannons, cannonballs, and sapper charges.
 Mechanics: Master builder of turrets, land mines, and Shredder mechsuits with sawblades and flamethrowers.
 Invention: Support healer placing effect beacons, deploying nanobots, and using a healing gun to purge and patch allies.
 Strengths and Weaknesses
 Understanding where this class excels helps you decide if it fits your preferred content and playstyle.
 Strength: Unique gadget gameplay
 Strength: Invention provides group healing
 Strength: Demolition strong area denial
 Weakness: Gadget and round management
 Weakness: Complex rotation
 Weakness: Lower sustained DPS than pure casters
 Leveling Tips
 Demolition offers the fastest leveling with strong AoE bombs. Invention shines in dungeons where beacons support the group. Manage gadget charges during extended fights.
