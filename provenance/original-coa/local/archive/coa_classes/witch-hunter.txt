 Witch Hunter Overview
 Witch Hunters are the monsters' worst nightmare — agile hunters purging evil across four specializations. Boltslinger relentlessly tracks targets with crossbow combos, Inquisition purges through faith and fire, Darkness hunts with shadow hounds and muskets, and Black Knight tanks in witchmail leeching health through dark strikes.
 Dark vigilante using dual crossbows, muskets, and glazed blades to purge Azeroth of wicked monsters.
 Class Mechanic — Judgment
 Witch Hunters purge evil with silver ammunition — agile monster hunters feared by all creatures of darkness.
 Witch Hunter uses Focus as its primary resource and wears Mail armor.
 Specializations
 At level 10, Witch Hunter players choose one of three specializations, each offering a distinct playstyle:
 Boltslinger: Mobile mid-range repeater crossbow DPS comboing silver-coated bolts in a deadly hunt flurry.
 Inquisition: Faith-driven purger alternating Purity and Wickedness to cauterize darkness in righteous or burning fire.
 Darkness: Shadow musketeer with silver-loaded musket and shadowy hounds tracking evil — no attachment to the light.
 Black Knight: Witchmail tank wielding dark magic and two-handed weapon strikes to leech health from enemies.
 Strengths and Weaknesses
 Understanding where this class excels helps you decide if it fits your preferred content and playstyle.
 Strength: Four specs with tank and DPS paths
 Strength: Boltslinger mobile arena threat
 Strength: Counters dark magic classes
 Weakness: Focus management
 Weakness: Black Knight lower DPS than pure tanks
 Weakness: Complex spec identities
 Leveling Tips
 Boltslinger provides consistent ranged leveling with mobility. Black Knight works for dungeon tanking once mechanics are learned. Inquisition excels against undead and dark caster mobs.
