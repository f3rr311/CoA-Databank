Cultist Class Guide — Conquest of Azeroth | July 2026 
 
 
 Cultist Overview
 Cultists serve N'zoth, Y'shaarj, C'thun, or Yogg-Saron, gaining different abilities per patron. The Insanity mechanic creates a risk-reward loop where pushing too far means certain death. With four specializations spanning tank, DPS, and healer, Cultists offer unmatched role flexibility among dark magic classes.
 Fanatic sworn to the Old Gods, balancing forbidden power against the madness that threatens to consume you.
 Class Mechanic — Insanity
 Cultists generate Insanity when using powerful abilities — higher insanity means more damage, but full insanity destroys you.
 Cultist uses Insanity as its primary resource and wears Cloth / Plate armor.
 Specializations
 At level 10, Cultist players choose one of three specializations, each offering a distinct playstyle:
 Godblade: Channel Old God power into your blade, summoning tentacles and dark runes for devastating unholy strikes.
 Corruption: Intellect caster inflicting shadow nightmares and DoTs, balancing Insanity for massive damage boosts.
 Influence: Dark healer swaying allies with Yogg-Saron absorb shields, healing, and Edict spells sharing unique procs.
 Dreadnought: Y'shaarj-powered tank absorbing insane punishment with heavy armor and powerful absorption effects.
 Strengths and Weaknesses
 Understanding where this class excels helps you decide if it fits your preferred content and playstyle.
 Strength: Four distinct roles across specs
 Strength: Scaling damage with Insanity
 Strength: Godblade and Dreadnought offer melee/tank paths
 Weakness: Self-destruction at max Insanity
 Weakness: Steep learning curve
 Weakness: Corruption requires careful resource management
 Leveling Tips
 Corruption provides predictable ranged damage for solo play. Monitor Insanity thresholds carefully. Influence works well in dungeon groups needing dark healing.
