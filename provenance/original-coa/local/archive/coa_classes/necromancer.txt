 Necromancer Overview
 The Necromancer is one of Conquest of Azeroth's most iconic classes, commanding the dead through Life Force. At level 10 you choose Death for disease spread, Animation for minion swarms, or Rime for frost-powered lichdom. Early levels introduce basic summons; the full pet army unlocks through the 30–60 bracket.
 Dark caster who commands hordes of undead minions — skeletons, wraiths, abominations, and ghouls — while wielding plague and frost damage.
 Class Mechanic — Life Force
 Necromancers possess a pool of reanimating energy used to raise and sustain undead pets.
 Necromancer uses Mana as its primary resource and wears Cloth armor.
 Specializations
 At level 10, Necromancer players choose one of three specializations, each offering a distinct playstyle:
 Death: Spread devastating diseases with plague bombs and crypt plagues for sustained DoT damage while minions batter diseased foes.
 Animation: Become a dark summoner, animating hordes of undead to swarm and overwhelm enemies.
 Rime: Embrace the frosts of Northrend, transforming into a powerful Lich with icy magic and caster-focused minions.
 Strengths and Weaknesses
 Understanding where this class excels helps you decide if it fits your preferred content and playstyle.
 Strength: Strong solo leveling with pets
 Strength: Excellent sustained AoE
 Strength: Multiple minion types for flexibility
 Weakness: Vulnerable to focus fire in PvP
 Weakness: Pet management adds complexity
 Weakness: Lower burst than pure casters
 Leveling Tips
 Prioritize Animation or Death for smooth solo questing. Keep minions active between pulls and use crowd control on humanoid targets. At level 30, invest Ability Essences into your spec tree for meaningful power spikes.
