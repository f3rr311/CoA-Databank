 Venomancer Overview
 Venomancers serve Shadra with poisonous shapeshifting forms. Rot erodes enemies with entropy poisons, Vizier heals through nerubian ritual magic, Stalking assassinates from spider burrows, and Fortitude tanks in scorpion form with molting exoskeleton defenses. Damage increases over fight duration as venom stacks accumulate.
 Disciple of the Poison Loa Shadra, shapeshifting into poisonous insects to erode enemies or heal allies.
 Class Mechanic — Venom Stacks
 Venomancers apply stacking poisons and shapeshift into spider, scorpion, snake, and other toxic forms.
 Venomancer uses Toxins as its primary resource and wears Leather / Mail armor.
 Specializations
 At level 10, Venomancer players choose one of three specializations, each offering a distinct playstyle:
 Rot: Rotweaver spreading poisons and toxic mayhem that hinder and debilitate foes across the battlefield.
 Vizier: Nerubian vizier healer mending allies through regenerative magic and Shadra's Vigil ritual chanting.
 Stalking: Spider-form assassin burrowing underground, progressing to Serpent Lord, Nerubian, or Spider Lord forms.
 Fortitude: Scorpion-form tank with natural armor, exoskeleton molting stages, and damage reflection.
 Strengths and Weaknesses
 Understanding where this class excels helps you decide if it fits your preferred content and playstyle.
 Strength: Four specs across all roles
 Strength: Strong DoT damage in Rot
 Strength: Fortitude unique poison tank
 Weakness: Ramp-up time for poison stacks
 Weakness: Shapeshift complexity
 Weakness: Dispel vulnerability on DoTs
 Leveling Tips
 Rot simplifies AoE questing with spread toxins. Stalking burrow helps with difficult elites. Vizier becomes essential in dungeon groups.
