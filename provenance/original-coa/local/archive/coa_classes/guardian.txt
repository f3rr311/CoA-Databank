 Guardian Overview
 Guardians are stoic knights committed to chivalry with strength-focused martial combat. Gladiator engages in brutal physical fights, Vanguard shields allies as the ultimate tank, and Inspiration supports the party as a battle bard with banners and songs. Guardians are essential for CoA's dungeon and raid content.
 Stoic knight defending allies and inspiring the party — a stalwart soldier for any adventuring group.
 Class Mechanic — Chivalry
 Guardians commit to chivalric combat rules, mastering all martial weapons with heavy plate defense.
 Guardian uses Resolve as its primary resource and wears Plate armor.
 Specializations
 At level 10, Guardian players choose one of three specializations, each offering a distinct playstyle:
 Gladiator: Savage fighter with nets, charges, and leaps sticking to foes — robust plate defenses with physical damage focus.
 Vanguard: Ultimate shield tank leading the charge with ironclad protection and AoE threat generation.
 Inspiration: Support bard placing empowering banners, singing battle songs, and bolstering group morale and power.
 Strengths and Weaknesses
 Understanding where this class excels helps you decide if it fits your preferred content and playstyle.
 Strength: Premier shield tank in Vanguard
 Strength: Inspiration unique Support spec
 Strength: Gladiator strong solo DPS
 Weakness: Lower DPS than pure melee
 Weakness: Inspiration requires group for full value
 Weakness: Multiple role complexity
 Leveling Tips
 Gladiator levels faster solo than Vanguard. Switch to Vanguard when joining dungeon groups. Inspiration shines in organized raid groups.
