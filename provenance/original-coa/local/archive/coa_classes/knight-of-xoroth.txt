 Knight of Xoroth Overview
 Knights of Xoroth are juggernauts of wrathful flame riding dark steeds into battle. War butchers with hooks and chains, Hellfire sears foes with demonic fire magic through your blade, and Defiance opens Hellgates to summon flaming demons as a sword-and-board tank. Deathfire finishing moves herald apocalyptic destruction.
 Savage Hell Knight riding a dark steed, decimating foes with blades, chains, hooks, and hellfire.
 Class Mechanic — Deathfire
 Knights of Xoroth build Deathfire in combat like combo points, unleashing apocalyptic finishing moves.
 Knight of Xoroth uses Deathfire as its primary resource and wears Plate armor.
 Specializations
 At level 10, Knight of Xoroth players choose one of three specializations, each offering a distinct playstyle:
 War: Physical butcher flaying enemies with hooks and chains, dragging foes close for health-regenerating kills.
 Hellfire: Hybrid hell knight channeling demonic fire through your blade with blazing demon transformation bursts.
 Defiance: Hellgate tank summoning imps and abyssal infernals from the firelands as an unstoppable flaming juggernaut.
 Strengths and Weaknesses
 Understanding where this class excels helps you decide if it fits your preferred content and playstyle.
 Strength: Hellfire massive AoE burst
 Strength: Defiance unique demon-summoning tank
 Strength: Mounted steed combat
 Weakness: Deathfire combo management
 Weakness: Hellfire gear hybrid complexity
 Weakness: Dark thematic niche
 Leveling Tips
 War charge-open on mobs with hook combos for burst damage. Hellfire hybrid gearing takes practice but delivers strong AoE. Defiance works for dungeon tanking once Hellgate mechanics are mastered.
