 Sun Cleric Overview
 Sun Clerics worship the physical sun, augmenting the Light with raw fire. Piety casts devastating holy-fire spells, Seraphim tanks or DPSes with radiant shields, Blessings heals through solar faith, and Valkyrie carves paths of righteous destruction in melee. Sunset stacks reward timing and create explosive burst moments.
 Paladin of the blazing sun wielding righteous fire to heal, protect, or smite enemies with divine fury.
 Class Mechanic — Sunset
 Certain abilities generate Solar Power; at 20 stacks, unleash it to modify spells with additional effects.
 Sun Cleric uses Solar Power as its primary resource and wears Cloth / Plate armor.
 Specializations
 At level 10, Sun Cleric players choose one of three specializations, each offering a distinct playstyle:
 Piety: Solar priest weaving burning light into powerful holy and fire spells with Sunset burst windows.
 Seraphim: Wrathful angel dual-wielding DPS or shield tank, infusing weapons and armor with burning light.
 Blessings: Healer using faith in flame to protect allies, building Solar Power for massive group healing.
 Valkyrie: Val'kyr-imbued dual-wielding melee unleashing devastating flurries of flaming spear strikes.
 Strengths and Weaknesses
 Understanding where this class excels helps you decide if it fits your preferred content and playstyle.
 Strength: Four roles from one class
 Strength: Plate durability on Seraphim/Valkyrie
 Strength: Sunset burst damage windows
 Weakness: Solar Power resource management
 Weakness: Complex spec selection
 Weakness: Multiple stat priorities across specs
 Leveling Tips
 Valkyrie or Seraphim DPS levels fastest solo with plate durability. Switch to Blessings when joining groups. Stack Sunset before spending for optimal burst.
