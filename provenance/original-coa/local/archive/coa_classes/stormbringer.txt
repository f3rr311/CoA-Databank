 Stormbringer Overview
 Stormbringers wield unbound lightning through the Static mechanic. Lightning delivers single-target burst, Thunder transforms the entire battlefield into a storm, and Winds supports allies with a growing Air Elemental that Unleashes for massive power spikes. Static stacking up to 100 creates dramatic burst windows.
 Lightning mage electrifying the battlefield with crackling storms of wind, thunder, and lightning.
 Class Mechanic — Static
 Stormbringer abilities generate Static stacking up to 100 times, increasing proc chances and ability damage.
 Stormbringer uses Static as its primary resource and wears Cloth armor.
 Specializations
 At level 10, Stormbringer players choose one of three specializations, each offering a distinct playstyle:
 Lightning: Single-target burst caster building Static to unleash additional effects on powerful lightning spells.
 Thunder: Ultimate AoE mage summoning lightning orbs, chain lightnings, and instant casts across the battlefield.
 Winds: Support spec summoning an Air Elemental that grows stronger in combat, buffing allies while devastating foes.
 Strengths and Weaknesses
 Understanding where this class excels helps you decide if it fits your preferred content and playstyle.
 Strength: Thunder exceptional AoE
 Strength: Winds unique Support elemental
 Strength: Static burst windows
 Weakness: Static stack management to 100
 Weakness: Cloth fragility
 Weakness: Lightning weaker on multi-target without Thunder
 Leveling Tips
 Thunder provides smooth ranged leveling with natural AoE. Build Static before engaging elites for burst finishers. Winds becomes valuable in dungeon groups.
