 Bloodmage Overview
 Sons of Arugal wrestle with Arugal's Worgen curse across four paths. Ferocity transforms into savage Worgen, Blood embraces San'layn sanguine magic, Packleader leads a permanent Worgen shadow pack, and Fleshweaver heals through blood rituals. The class alternates between caster and enraged wolf — mastering the curse defines your identity.
 Bearer of the sanguine Worgen curse — torn between feral bloodlust and crimson Blood Mage mastery.
 Class Mechanic — Worgen Curse
 Sons of Arugal grapple with the Worgen curse, alternating between feral wolf and blood magic caster forms.
 Bloodmage uses Rage / Vitality as its primary resource and wears Leather armor.
 Specializations
 At level 10, Bloodmage players choose one of three specializations, each offering a distinct playstyle:
 Ferocity: Give into the curse, building rage through blood magic to transform into a ferocious Worgen tearing foes apart.
 Blood: Embrace the San'layn gift using your own health to devastate enemies and transfuse life into allies.
 Packleader: Permanently transform into Worgen commanding a Shadow Pack that summons shadow worgen in combat.
 Fleshweaver: Blood Mage healer weaving rejuvenating spells, pooling Vitality for powerful blood rituals that mend mortal wounds.
 Strengths and Weaknesses
 Understanding where this class excels helps you decide if it fits your preferred content and playstyle.
 Strength: Four specs spanning all roles
 Strength: Ferocity strong melee burst
 Strength: Fleshweaver unique blood healer
 Weakness: Form and resource management
 Weakness: Blood spec self-damage risk
 Weakness: Complex curse mechanics
 Leveling Tips
 Ferocity simplifies early leveling with strong self-sustain in Worgen form. Blood works in groups as a life-transfusing DPS. Practice form transitions during combat.
