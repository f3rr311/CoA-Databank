 Barbarian Overview
 Barbarians are CoA's most approachable class with Unbridled Rage driving enraged burst windows. Brutality delivers satisfying melee cleave, Headhunting throws axes and spears from range, and Ancestry buffs allies through Vrykul heritage. Share drinks with allies for temporary group buffs in a raging good time.
 Savage berserker cleaving foes with axes and spears, sharing ale with allies for temporary buffs.
 Class Mechanic — Unbridled Rage
 Barbarians build rage in combat and become Enraged after critical strikes — certain abilities require enraged state.
 Barbarian uses Rage as its primary resource and wears Leather armor.
 Specializations
 At level 10, Barbarian players choose one of three specializations, each offering a distinct playstyle:
 Ancestry: Call on Vrykul divine ancestors to buff allies while carving foes with a frost axe and ale-fueled celebrations.
 Brutality: Unstoppable savage fighter tearing through foes with massive cleave using 2H weapons or dual wield.
 Headhunting: Throw axes and spears from distance, unleashing deadly bleeds before enemies reach you.
 Strengths and Weaknesses
 Understanding where this class excels helps you decide if it fits your preferred content and playstyle.
 Strength: Simplest class to learn
 Strength: Brutality strong AoE cleave
 Strength: Ancestry Support buffs for groups
 Weakness: Limited crowd control
 Weakness: Headhunting requires kiting skill
 Weakness: Lower sustain than other melee
 Leveling Tips
 Brutality is the recommended beginner spec — simple rotation, strong AoE, leather armor. Build rage on packs before using enraged abilities.
