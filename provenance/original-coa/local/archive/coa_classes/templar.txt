 Templar Overview
 Templars are agile martial artists mastering combo chains. Crusader delivers holy light blade vortexes, Martial eliminates enemies in flurry strikes with combo-enhanced effects, and Discipline tanks with staff deflections and stacking mitigation. Openers, Followups, and Combo Breakers define the skill ceiling.
 Devout martial artist chaining combo strikes with blade, staff, or fists before unleashing finishing moves.
 Class Mechanic — Combo
 Templars weave Openers and Followups into Combat Moves, finishing foes with Combo Breaker abilities.
 Templar uses Conviction as its primary resource and wears Mail armor.
 Specializations
 At level 10, Templar players choose one of three specializations, each offering a distinct playstyle:
 Crusader: Graceful holy fighter weaving in and out of range with spiritual blade vortexes and light-infused strikes.
 Martial: Flurry striker utilizing powerful combos to unleash devastating effects in swift successive strikes.
 Discipline: Staff-wielding monk tank deflecting blows and maintaining a stacking mitigation buff through woven strikes.
 Strengths and Weaknesses
 Understanding where this class excels helps you decide if it fits your preferred content and playstyle.
 Strength: Combo system skill expression
 Strength: Discipline reliable staff tank
 Strength: Crusader holy melee fantasy
 Weakness: Combo order strictness
 Weakness: Conviction resource management
 Weakness: Lower burst if combos break
 Leveling Tips
 Martial levels efficiently with strong single-target combo chains. Learn your Opener-Followup sequences before specializing at level 10. Discipline becomes the go-to tank spec for dungeon groups.
