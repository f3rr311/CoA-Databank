 Runemaster Overview
 Runemasters inscribe runes and cycle elemental Attunements for wild magical effects. Spellslinger delivers pure arcane devastation, Riftblade assassinates with elemental blade combos, and Conjuration plays around a Runic Familiar with displacement tactics. Inscribe Rune stores up to four runes for mana regeneration and empowered casts.
 Rune-inscribing arcanist weaving wild magic through ancient glyphs and a bonded familiar.
 Class Mechanic — Attunement
 Runemasters cycle Attunement between Frost, Fire, and Arcane every 8 seconds, storing up to 4 runes for mana and ability power.
 Runemaster uses Runes as its primary resource and wears Cloth armor.
 Specializations
 At level 10, Runemaster players choose one of three specializations, each offering a distinct playstyle:
 Spellslinger: Raw arcane caster using shifting wild magic to inscribe and unleash devastating Thaumaturgy spells.
 Riftblade: Agile assassin cycling Fire, Frost, and Arcane blades within a Mana Shroud for combo spell devastation.
 Conjuration: Pet-focused spec where all abilities radiate from your Runic Familiar with teleports and shockwaves.
 Strengths and Weaknesses
 Understanding where this class excels helps you decide if it fits your preferred content and playstyle.
 Strength: Unique familiar gameplay in Conjuration
 Strength: Riftblade combo depth
 Strength: Attunement cycling adds tactical layer
 Weakness: Familiar dependency in Conjuration
 Weakness: Attunement timing complexity
 Weakness: Cloth fragility
 Leveling Tips
 Spellslinger is straightforward for solo leveling. Conjuration requires familiar positioning practice but becomes powerful once mastered. Inscribe runes before engaging elites.
