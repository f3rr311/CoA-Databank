 Starcaller Overview
 Starcallers bring celestial power to CoA with four specializations spanning every role. Warden tanks as an Elune avatar, Sentinel delivers moonbow DPS or healing, Moon Priest focuses on restoration, and Moon Guard protects with arcane barriers. Few classes match this role flexibility.
 Astral knight infused with Elune's lunar power, adapting to any role with blade, bow, or moon magic.
 Class Mechanic — Constellation
 Starcallers align with celestial patterns, gaining bonuses based on active constellation configurations.
 Starcaller uses Starlight as its primary resource and wears Mail / Plate armor.
 Specializations
 At level 10, Starcaller players choose one of three specializations, each offering a distinct playstyle:
 Warden: Walk Maiev's path as an arcane-empowered avatar of Elune charging into battle.
 Sentinel: Priestess of the Moon enhancing bows with astral magic — ranged DPS or healer with warsaber mounted combat.
 Moon Priest: Weave moonlight to restore allies, using enchanted arrows and lunar phases for mana and healing.
 Moon Guard: Elite Kaldorei sorcerer using arcane barriers that gain strength from mana flow to protect allies.
 Strengths and Weaknesses
 Understanding where this class excels helps you decide if it fits your preferred content and playstyle.
 Strength: Four specs covering tank, DPS, and heal
 Strength: Unique cosmic theme
 Strength: Sentinel and Moon Priest fill Support roles
 Weakness: Constellation management overhead
 Weakness: Complex for new players
 Weakness: Mail/plate hybrid gearing
 Leveling Tips
 Sentinel Moonbow works for solo ranged content. Moon Priest becomes essential in dungeon groups. Track constellation timers during boss fights.
