 Felsworn Overview
 Felsworn repurpose demonic knowledge for a greater cause. Slaying combos melee attacks for burst damage, Infernal channels chaos magic as a mobile caster, and Tyrant permanently transforms into a demon tank. Felfury Charges cycle on a timer, and Demon Form at max rage grants additional damage.
 Former Burning Legion agent wielding demonic knowledge — agile fighter cleaving foes or channeling chaos magic.
 Class Mechanic — Demon Form
 Felsworn gather rage to transform into Demon Form, using Felfury Charges and Felstrikes for fel-enhanced combat.
 Felsworn uses Felfury as its primary resource and wears Leather armor.
 Specializations
 At level 10, Felsworn players choose one of three specializations, each offering a distinct playstyle:
 Slaying: Highly mobile melee combo fighter unleashing brutal attacks in specific orders for bonus effects and burst.
 Infernal: Chaos Mage conjuring felfire storms and instant-cast DoTs, dashing around the battlefield bathing foes in felfire.
 Tyrant: Permanent Demon-form tank wielding felfire and flames while evading and deflecting damage.
 Strengths and Weaknesses
 Understanding where this class excels helps you decide if it fits your preferred content and playstyle.
 Strength: Slaying mobile combo burst
 Strength: Infernal agile chaos caster
 Strength: Tyrant unique demon tank
 Weakness: Demon Form uptime management
 Weakness: Felstrike proc dependency
 Weakness: Morally dark theme
 Leveling Tips
 Slaying levels quickly with mobile combo attacks. Tyrant provides safer leveling with demon tank durability. Track Felfury Charge cycles during extended fights.
