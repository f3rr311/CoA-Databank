 Ranger Overview
 Rangers are scouts and brigands of the wilderness with bushcraft survival skills. Archery peppers foes from afar with quiver-modified arrows, Brigand ambushes with poisons and dirty tricks, and Farstrider supports allies with war falcons and hunting horns. Farstrider represents CoA's new Support role category.
 Wilderness outlaw proficient with bow and blade, allied with war falcons and hunting horns.
 Class Mechanic — Bushcraft
 Rangers use bushcraft skills — quiver types, camp sites, and war falcons — to hunt and support allies.
 Ranger uses Focus as its primary resource and wears Leather armor.
 Specializations
 At level 10, Ranger players choose one of three specializations, each offering a distinct playstyle:
 Archery: Dedicated long-range archer using Quiver abilities to modify damage with excellent mobility.
 Brigand: Ambush bandit assaulting with brutal melee, poisons, and dirty tricks from civilization's edge.
 Farstrider: Support spec calling War Falcons and blowing hunting horns to buff allies — a CoA Support role.
 Strengths and Weaknesses
 Understanding where this class excels helps you decide if it fits your preferred content and playstyle.
 Strength: Archery familiar hunter gameplay
 Strength: Brigand strong melee ambush
 Strength: Farstrider unique Support role
 Weakness: Focus and quiver management
 Weakness: Brigand lower range than Archery
 Weakness: Farstrider needs group for full value
 Leveling Tips
 Archery is the premier solo leveling spec with quiver flexibility. Switch quiver types for different mob types — poison for armored elites, swift for casters. Farstrider shines in dungeon groups.
