 Primalist Overview
 Primalists channel primordial powers across four specializations. Geomancy devastates with earthbending, Mountain King tanks with tectonic reflection, Wildwalker summons spirit animals as a totem warrior, and Life heals as a melee rage-based juggernaut. Maintaining the natural order while empowering allies defines the class fantasy.
 Speaker of the earth commanding geomancy, mountain spirits, wild gods, and the Earthmother's life magic.
 Class Mechanic — Totems
 Primalists channel earth, nature, and beast spirits, augmenting themselves and allies with primal boons.
 Primalist uses Primal Energy as its primary resource and wears Plate armor.
 Specializations
 At level 10, Primalist players choose one of three specializations, each offering a distinct playstyle:
 Geomancy: Embodiment of earthen fury shaping earth and bludgeoning enemies with shards of stone.
 Mountain King: Defensive bastion of might and earth magic with Tectonic Shift reflecting damage — but overloading stuns you.
 Wildwalker: Totem warrior communing with wild gods, summoning Spirit Animals and fighting with 2H or dual wield.
 Life: Melee rage-based healer invoking the Earthmother with radiating AoE heals and animalistic boons.
 Strengths and Weaknesses
 Understanding where this class excels helps you decide if it fits your preferred content and playstyle.
 Strength: Four specs covering all roles
 Strength: Plate armor durability
 Strength: Unique earth/nature fantasy
 Weakness: Totem and tectonic management
 Weakness: Mountain King self-stun risk
 Weakness: Split stat priorities
 Leveling Tips
 Wildwalker provides smooth melee leveling with spirit pets. Geomancy works for ranged earth damage. Life becomes essential in dungeon groups at higher levels.
