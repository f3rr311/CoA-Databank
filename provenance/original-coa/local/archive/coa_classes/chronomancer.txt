 Chronomancer Overview
 Chronomancers manipulate the timeways through three distinct paths. Duality balances Order and Chaos magic, Artificer empowers wands with Bronze Flight magic for ramping auto-attack builds, and Displacement rewinds ally health with wild utility spells. Time Warp creates dramatic reversal moments unlike anything in traditional WoW.
 Time mage bending temporal forces to unmake foes, remake wounded allies, and alter space-time.
 Class Mechanic — Time Warp
 Chronomancers manipulate time flow — rewinding damage, accelerating allies, or slowing enemies.
 Chronomancer uses Temporal Energy as its primary resource and wears Cloth armor.
 Specializations
 At level 10, Chronomancer players choose one of three specializations, each offering a distinct playstyle:
 Duality: Balance Order direct/AoE damage with Chaos erosion and execution damage for devastating dual-magic combos.
 Artificer: Bronze Flight wand specialist building attack speed for torrential auto-attack magic barrages.
 Displacement: Support healer rewinding ally health with Aeon-modified heals and a Bronze Whelp pet assistant.
 Strengths and Weaknesses
 Understanding where this class excels helps you decide if it fits your preferred content and playstyle.
 Strength: Unique time manipulation
 Strength: Displacement powerful healer
 Strength: Artificer ramping wand DPS
 Weakness: Complex mechanics
 Weakness: Duality requires dual-magic balance
 Weakness: Cloth fragility
 Leveling Tips
 Artificer provides adequate solo damage once wand mechanics are understood. Displacement becomes valuable in dungeon groups. Practice Time Warp timing on elite mobs.
