[overview]
Grovekeeper is an easy to play DPS/Healer hybrid that summons Treants whenever it melee critically strikes, which explode for AoE damage and healing. It reaches peak power at lvl 57 when you unlock the Treant talent, but it is still very good before that point. It can top DPS and healing meters just by pressing 2 or 3 buttons, and provides powerful buffs such as Bloodlust and 20% crit chance. Your party will love you for playing this class.

[talents]
Rock Barrier can be taken instead of Mineralization for solo play.

[rotation]
Spam Wildclaw to proc as many treants as possible, cast an instant cast Earthquake once every 9 seconds, use Seismic Crash if you need to generate rage and to upkeep the spell hit chance buff for your party. Cast Seismic Wave in AoE. That's pretty much it.  Cast Primal Awakening (Bloodlust) and Tree of War along with your stat buff totem when your party needs to burst down a powerful enemy.

[stats]
Crit Chance is really the only thing that matters, as treants are the bulk of your dps and healing and you want to spawn as many as possible. Crit > Strength > Haste is your stat priority. While leveling and as a fresh lvl 60, I would recommend using a 2-handed weapon as it helps generate bonus rage. Slower weapons hit harder with Wild Strike, but faster weapons let you spawn more Treants. Once you have extremely good gear and have solved your rage problems you can consider swapping to fast dual-wielded onehanders, as they will let you spawn many more Treants.
