[overview]
This is an AoE-focused build with decent single-target for bosses with CDs. I've only been playing Primalist since the project started and have tried min-maxing different builds for each melee spec.

[talents]
Key talents here are Pulverize, Elemental Berserker, Totemic Echoes, Torn to Shreds, Spiritual Frenzy, Wild Carnage, and Totem Warrior.

Flex talents can be Wild At Heart; this talent is only good if you can keep your rage up to spam Wildclaws fast enough. If you need more single-target damage for raids, remove Wild Carnage with Rylak's Blessing.

[rotation]
Key Rotation:

Open with Grove Guardian>Totemic Smash> Primal Shred>Boulder Dash>Seismic Spike. Then spam Wildclaws until Totemic Smash is off CD.
 
Maintain Primal Shred and Torn to Shreds on all targets for rage/procs, and keep Wild Carnage buffed up every 6s by using Seismic Spike.

ST: Same opener, but instead of Seismic Spike, use Seismic Crash unless you need to cleave. Pop Primal Convergence and Savage Frenzy to increase DPS. 

*Grove Guardians is uncapped aoe dps and should always be taken.

[stats]
Attack Power and Strength will be your top stats. 

Crit will be your best secondary stat since we use Wildclaws as a substitute for attack speed.

Be sure to get hit capped and some expertise.*




* Still working on bis gear list and stats.

[tips]
I highly recommend running your pet on passive and macro /petattack into your key ST abilities.

BIS Pet: Wolf; it has a 20s AOE attack power buff on a 40s CD and an additional 20s 10% attack power buff on a 5-minute CD.
