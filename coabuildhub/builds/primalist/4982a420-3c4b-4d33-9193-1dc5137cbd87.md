[overview]
This is an AoE-focused healing build with decent single-target for bosses with CDs. I've only been playing Primalist since the project started and have tried min-maxing different builds for each melee spec.

*I run this build with my guild as 4 DPS + 1 Tank. You need someone to queue as a healer and play DPS for you since we cannot queue as a healer ourselves.

[talents]
Key talents here are Nature's Blessing, Ancient of War, Sacred Grove, and Earthmother's Roar.

Flex talents can be Ride the Wave and Earthmother's Empowerment for faster dungeon running.

[rotation]
Key Rotation:

Use Primal Awakening on pull. (Lust)

Open with Primal Rush (on your party)>Spirit Charge (on your tank)>Grove Guardian>Seismic Wave>Earthquake proc. Then spam Wildclaws until Seismic Wave is off CD.

Maintain Flourishing Growth (on party) and Heavy-Handed (on tank). Maintain at least 1 Earthquake on the ground.

Spot heal as needed with Hand of the Earthmother. Emergency heal with Sacred Grove and Protective Roar.

*Grove Guardian is uncapped AoE DPS/Healing and should always be taken.

[stats]
BIS Stats are going off of bisbeard.com

Strength, Attack Power, Crit, and Agility are all going to be top for this build.

Any crafted item with the of the Champion prefix.
