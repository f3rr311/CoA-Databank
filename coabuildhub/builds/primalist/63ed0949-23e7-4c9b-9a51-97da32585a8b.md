[overview]
This is an AoE-focused build with decent single-target for bosses with CDs. I've only been playing Primalist since the project started and have tried min-maxing different builds for each melee spec.

I use this build to do all of Scarlet Monastery - Graveyard in 2 pulls. It is very easy to farm Marks of Triumph this way.

[talents]
Key talents here are Primal Guardian, Raging Earth, Mountain Fury, and Earthen Avatar.

Flex talents can be any talent that requires one hand or an offhand. I play using two hands, so some talents are only used to get to later talents.

[rotation]
Key Rotation:

Open with Quake>Seismic Spike>Earthquake proc>Mountain Fury to group adds. Then spam Hand of the Earthmother on yourself until Quake is off CD.

Maintain Primal Guardian and Heavy-Handed. Maintain at least 1 Earthquake on the ground.

Defensive rotations are Primal Convergence, Bearskin, Rock Barrier (only use when Primal Guardian is falling off), and Mountain Fury.

*Grove Guardians is uncapped AoE DPS/Healing and should always be taken.
