[talents]
Dark infusion didn't feel good in my opinion, also very cost inefficien. Dark veil only to be used as a last resort.

[rotation]
Always keep 2x black blood up
Depending on mana regen of party blanket the group in void shields, split mind makes you apply 2
Always keep up Void Shield on tank
Keep up Sermon of Dread
Tentacle at 75+ sanity
Try to time Entropic slam/ Blade of the Empire with tank dmg intake since it's your biggest spot heals
Forbidden ritual few seconds before aoe damage.
Gaze of C'thun as big instant (Due to Void-Touched) group heal.
Hallucination and Isolate to drop threat while following the tank during pull, can also Isolate a dps to save them.
Abyssal Ward and Embrace the void as personals.
EM doesn't heal for that much, use it maybe if you need an extra spot heal on a dps or dont have anything else.

Keep mana between 20 - 60

Haste Presence

Hand of Yogg could be a possibility later but haven't felt the need to use it because I already feel quite gcd starved.

Dps rotation: Slam Blade Slam Blade Slam Blade Blade Slam

[stats]
Crit > SP > STR > haste with enough INT to feel comfortable not having to sanity tap every 3 gcd's. You want a healthy balance between crit and sp.

[gear]
Best case scenario would be plate, STR/INT/STAM, crit/sp.

[leveling]
prio int/sp early, slow 2h. Run right behind the tank unless he's pulling back.
