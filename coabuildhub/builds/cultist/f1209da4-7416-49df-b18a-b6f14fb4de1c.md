[overview]
Works well both if PvP and PvE. Few tweaks could be done to optimize for PVE.

[talents]
We take damage reductions nodes to better survive incoming damage passively and gives us chance to offheal some of incoming damage. 

Voidborne is a pretty solid offensive cooldown, 20 seconds of stun immunity is crazy. Could opt into Nutcase and Book of N'Zoth for move passive damage, but having good CDs is important in PvP.

[rotation]
In PvP, try to manage insanity so that we dont go into the 50% More damage taken state. You can purposly go over 100 insanity if you have End Times up vs other Melee classes.

Rifted Hammers of twilight will do most of our damage, so try to reduce it´s cd whenevers possible.

In PvE, try to go insane as often as possible, build up insanity as the pack gathers, Rift + HoT on cd, and otherwise spam Entropic slam or Hot in single target.

Use Voidborne on CD

[stats]
Str/Crit>Int>Agi

[gear]
Big damage slow 2h weapon for big bonks

[tips]
Enjoy bonking, use your Cds often!
