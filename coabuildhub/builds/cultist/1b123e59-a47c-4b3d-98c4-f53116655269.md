### Cultist / Heretic Build Summary

#### Build Philosophy
The gameplay relies on a constant synergy between damage and healing. You convert your aggression into survival for your group, with the absolute priority of keeping the tank alive via *Abyssal Covenant*.

#### Stat Priority and Gear Optimization
* **Primary Stats:** Your absolute priorities are Intellect and Spell Power. Intellect is the most critical stat ("the heart of the war").
* **Leveling Gear:** During the leveling phase, ignore armor types (cloth, leather, etc.). Focus solely on upgrades if an item offers more Intellect or Spell Power. If you have to choose between the two, find a balance based on your preferences (for example, losing 1 Intellect to gain 5 Spell Power is a worthwhile trade).
* **Weaponry:** Aim for the slowest possible attack speed and the highest base damage range. Using a two-handed weapon with Intellect and Attack Power is excellent, as Attack Power is converted into Spell Power for this build.

#### Priority Order and Combat Rotation
* **Tank Management:** Cast *Abyssal Covenant* at the start of the donjon to redirect the majority of your healing toward the tank.
* **Insanity Generation:** 
  * *Blade of the Empire* is your primary source of healing and insanity generation.
  * When you run out of *Blade of the Empire* charges, use *Malevolence* to continue generating insanity.
  * Combined with *Eldritch Eye*, aim to maximize critical strikes to make your spells instant and maintain maximum fluidity.
* **Damage Phase:** As soon as you have the required insanity, trigger *Entropic Slam* to deal maximum damage.
* **"Black Blood" Synergy:** Powered by your auto-attacks (at 100%) and your tentacles' damage (at 30%).

#### Group Management and Emergencies
* **Leveling:** Use *Tentacles of Yogg-Saron* for support. Once both charges are obtained, they become essential.
* **Emergency Healing:** **Gaze of C'thun** is your immediate relief resource for the group. If you see health getting critical, drop a **Gaze of C'thun** and be done with it.
* **Healing Rotation:** Alternate between 3 damage abilities, then use an instant heal to stabilize the group without interruption.
* **Bouncing Heal:** Use *Dark Infusion* (similar to a "Prayer of Mending") to heal by bouncing heals between allies.

#### Optimization and Survival
* **Stats:** Prioritize *Whisper of Fate* (12% stats) over pure mana regeneration.
* **Threat Management:** Reduce your threat with *Isolate* or *Hallucination* to avoid pulling aggro.
* **Presence:** Use the presence of *Y'shaarj* or **Gaze of C'thun**.

[talents]
#### Talent Synergies
* **Malevolence (Talent):** This is the starting point of your talent tree. It defines your group healer role. Its synergy is direct with other talents that amplify the impact of this spell to maintain allies' health under all circumstances.
* **Blade of Yogg-Saron (Talent):** This talent is the catalyst of your rotation. It directly enhances *Blade of the Empire*. Its synergy with your build is essential: it transforms a standard spell into your primary single-target healing tool (for the tank), while guaranteeing the resource generation needed to fuel the rest of your cycle.
* **Eldritch Eye (Talent):** This talent modifies the casting mechanic of your spells. By making your group heals instant, it creates a perfect synergy with your need for responsiveness. It allows you to bypass cast times to react to spike damage on the group.
* **Forbidden Ritual (Talent):** This talent adds an extra layer of survivability. It is designed to be the "panic button" that stabilizes the group when your other healing synergies are under pressure.

#### Overall Synergy Synthesis
The build relies on the interaction between *Blade of Yogg-Saron* (which optimizes your single-target healing by improving your spells) and *Eldritch Eye* (which makes your group healing more fluid by removing cast times). Everything is supported by your offensive cycles, creating a loop where every talent point is invested to optimize either responsiveness (instant cast) or raw power (single-target/group healing).

#### Core Mechanics (Combat Loop)
* **Fight Entry:** Use *Malevolence* right at the start of the fight to cast your first spells and apply group effects.
* **Blade of the Empire:** This is the engine of your build. It is your primary single-target healing source, essential for prioritizing the tank's health. Its cooldown is reset by your numerous critical strikes, making your crit score vital for rotation fluidity.
* **Eldritch Eye:** Use instantly whenever you can for your group spells to maintain responsiveness.
* **Dark Infusion:** Use as a bouncing heal if needed.
* **Forbidden Ritual:** Anticipate and cast if you feel heavy damage coming, as it requires cast time.
* **Insanity Generation:** Use this resource to power your offensive and utility spells throughout the combat.

#### Offensive and Healing Synergies
* **Malevolence:** Simply applies *Black Blood*.
* **Black Blood (Buff):** This effect is directly powered by your **auto-attacks (at 100%)** and **damage dealt by your tentacles (at 30%)**. Your melee gameplay and summons keep this buff alive and progressing.
* **Group Recovery:** To efficiently recover the group's health, using **Gaze of C'thun** remains the best option.
* **Eldritch Eye:** This talent allows you to cast your spells instantly to guarantee maximum responsiveness during group healing.
Mana and Sanity Tap Management

* Combat opening: At the start of the dungeon or when pulling a group, while your pool is full, cast your spells freely to immediately set up your synergy and secure the initial damage and healing.
* The target threshold (30% - 50%): Your permanent goal is to keep your mana reserve between 30% and 50% minimum. Do not remain passive waiting to run low. As soon as your consumption starts dragging your gauge down toward this zone, weave Sanity Tap directly into your cycle to stabilize your resource.
* Anticipating emergencies: Why aim for this 30% to 50% bracket? Because a dungeon fight can sometimes turn sour: the group will take heavy unexpected damage, and you will need to chain continuous emergency heals (Gaze of C'thun, instant spells, etc.). During these critical windows, you simply won't have time to cast Sanity Tap.
* Golden rule: Maintaining this 30% to 50% mana safety cushion guarantees that you can weather a spike in group damage without running out of resources at the worst possible moment.

[gear]
#### Gear and Stat Priority
* **Stat Priority (Intellect vs. Spell Power):** My priority is to maximize Intellect on every piece of gear. Intellect is the central stat because it directly increases your critical strike score. I only replace Intellect with Spell Power (SP) if the trade-off is minimal (for example, losing 3 to 5 Intellect for +10 SP). Any larger change is not worth the loss in critical strike potential and mana pool.
* **Weapon Selection (Leveling):** During the leveling phase, a staff is mandatory to get the necessary amount of Intellect. You should look for a staff offering the maximum possible Intellect and Spell Power.
* **Damage Mechanics:** On your weapon, priority is given to the damage range. Since healing is converted from damage dealt, a higher damage range directly translates to more powerful heals.
* **Weapon Speed:** Always aim for the slowest possible attack speed. A slower attack speed guarantees higher damage per hit, which maximizes your total healing done.
