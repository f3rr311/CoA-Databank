[rotation]
## Opener

- Glacial Ward
- Greater Grim Mandate (Greater Foul Mandate if you already have a Grim Mandate active)
- Greater Razorice & Greater Chill of the Tomb (Optional)
- 3 [Animate: Crypt Fiend](coa-skill:necromancer:801941)
- Cap Runic Power with [Glacial Tap](coa-skill:necromancer:805369) or Runic Harvest
- [Animate: Skeletal Archer](coa-skill:necromancer:805040)
**Boss Gets Pulled**
- [Harvest Plague](coa-skill:necromancer:501890)
- Call of the Grave
- [Animate: Plaguefather](coa-skill:necromancer:805048)
- [Animate: Tomb King](coa-skill:necromancer:805044)
- [Animate: Frost Wyrm](coa-skill:necromancer:805428)
- [Unholy Frenzy](coa-skill:necromancer:805029)
- Spam Command: Undead until free instant cast from Bone King
- [Blight](coa-skill:necromancer:500217)
- Keep uptime on [Animate: Skeletal Archer](coa-skill:necromancer:805040), [Harvest Plague](coa-skill:necromancer:501890), and [Blight](coa-skill:necromancer:500217) when debuff wears off.
- Build with [Glacial Tap](coa-skill:necromancer:805369), Runic Harvest and, [Crypt Swarm](coa-skill:necromancer:500965) but dont overcap yourself with it.
- Spend on Command: Undead
- If free procs from Bone King and it wont overcap your runic power and you already have [Blight](coa-skill:necromancer:500217), spend it on [Lichfrost](coa-skill:necromancer:801722).
- Spawn [Animate: Tomb King](coa-skill:necromancer:805044) and [Animate: Skeletal Archer](coa-skill:necromancer:805040) when off CD.
- When [Animate: Plaguefather](coa-skill:necromancer:805048), [Animate: Tomb King](coa-skill:necromancer:805044), [Animate: Frost Wyrm](coa-skill:necromancer:805428) are all lining up again, Use Call of the Grave to make them instant free casts.
