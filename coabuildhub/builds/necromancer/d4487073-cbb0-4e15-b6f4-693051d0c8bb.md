[overview]
dungeon leveling build - get march of the dead - learn how to use it - win

stat prio:
leveling: Spell Power -> Intellect -> Stamina -> Spirit
50+: Spell Power!! -> Haste Rating! -> Intellect

mana regen on gear is a nice bonus if u have mp issues

minions:
dungeons - [2x] decaying colossus> abomination> crypt fiend> skeletal warrior
fill up with greater skeletal warrior for armour reduction
once u hit 60 go double bonk decaying colossus.

[talents]
March of the Dead - massive AoE damage ability. its clunky af tho, treat it as 4 second delay ground explosion. what helped me is placing it right below the target mobs once the tank has settled (imagine a rectangle floor spawn in front of player pawn) and dont think about how theyd walk into enemies for damage. once u get the hang of it and the hang of dungeon flow (tank pulls etc) u can anticipate where the mobs will end up. this is huge for damage.

requires 0 gear to pump serious dps. 1 or 2 MotD during a fight can easily make out 70% of ur dps, surpassing other within a blink. setting up however can be very frustrating if u have a tank that constantly moves or twitches around (mr felsworn :)). in that case gl.
standard single target damage rotation also hits hard when combined with Deaths Due (20% enemy hp proc).

[rotation]
buffs:
- grim mandate> foul mandate
- glacial ward> fetid ward/ bone ward

keep up at all times:
- glacial tap (if mana is buffed or u have no issues)
- animate: skeletal archer
- harvest plague on 1-2 enemies to spawn skeletons

general:
- command: hook for interrupt (can also be used to position mobs)
^-spec out of it once u get the decaying colossus
- tomb king for dmg
- plague father for defensive / all out
- when command: undead procs use blight on high hp enemies, otherwise use lichfrost
- runic harvest in between fights for extra resource 
- deaths due for extra dmg at low enemy health

- command: undead is ur main prio for single target and filler for AoE. make sure to constantly generate runic power (crypt swarm, glacial tap, etc.). ideally u build up runic power before u activate tomb king + frenzy -> command: undead
- march of the dead nr1 prio for AoE dmg. use glacial tap and crypt swarm to generate runic power inbetween

- make sure to create frozen reliquaries and keep them on cooldown when noticing mana isnt keeping up
- if mana is still an issue refrain from using heavy mana spenders on cd (glacial tap)
