[overview]
This build focuses more on AoE damage using 5 [Raise: Ghoul](coa-skill:necromancer:500971) and March of the Dead instead of big single target boss damage like my other guide. This build works VERY well with a tank that likes to pull big and preferably LoS the mobs around a corner or against a wall. Also is great for leveling in dungeons if you have people who can do single target damage.

[rotation]
## Opener

- Glacial Ward
- Greater Grim Mandate (Greater Foul Mandate if you already have Grim Mandate from someone else)
- Greater Razorice & Greater Chill of the Tomb (Optional)
- 5 [Raise: Ghoul](coa-skill:necromancer:500971)
- Cap Runic Power with [Glacial Tap](coa-skill:necromancer:805369) or Runic Harvest
**Once tank plants**
- Call of the Grave
- [Animate: Skeletal Archer](coa-skill:necromancer:805040) (Big 25% haste buff)
- [Animate: Bone Wraith](coa-skill:necromancer:805032)
- [Animate: Plaguefather](coa-skill:necromancer:805048)
- [Animate: Frost Wyrm](coa-skill:necromancer:805428)
- [Unholy Frenzy](coa-skill:necromancer:805029)
- March of the Dead (3x)
- [Glacial Tap](coa-skill:necromancer:805369)
- Runic Harvest
- March of the Dead (3x)
(Rinse and Repeat)
You may need to use [Crypt Swarm](coa-skill:necromancer:500965) for building Runic Power.

This rotation works very well if tank pulls massive and goes behind a wall, plant yourself so when the March of the Dead spawns, they will take like 1 or 2 steps and either collide with a wall or the pack of mobs (the tighter the pack, the better).

Another option is not using March of the Dead and focusing on your Command: Undead for your ghouls to do your AoE. Practically giving you infinite mana if the pull is big enough. Runic Harvest works a lot better with these ghouls since there is more to drain from.

[leveling]
Early leveling, I recommend 1 [Raise: Abomination](coa-skill:necromancer:500335) with rest being Skeletal Warrior. Once you unlock [Raise: Ghoul](coa-skill:necromancer:500971) you will swap all your summons to just Ghouls. They give you insane self healing since each auto they do heals you and each Command: Undead you get even more healing, turning excess healing into an absorb shield making you feel unkillable thanks to [Life For Power](coa-skill:necromancer:705746).
