Still working through this build, open to suggestions. Please comment with any suggestions or questions about the build.

[talents]
10-19: Rush Living Dead and Ghouls taking Bone King over Graverobber to help with early mana issues. 
20-?: From here you have a few options. Rush March of the Dead if you want a MASSIVE AOE ability for big pulls (~5+ targets).... hold off on Lich's Prodigy until you have Overwhelming Force and Lich Commander. Rush OF and LC ASAP to help with mana and RP efficiency. Frozen Reliquary will be a massive help too but may be expensive as corpse dust costs can stack up if you're leaning on it too heavily in the early game. 
Any defensive options shown are lowest priority and strictly taken to fill out the tree and open access to the last 2 talents. 

Decaying Colossus can be swapped for Foul Invocation as both are defensive options. Haven't tested DC against abom but considering he uses a frontal (that we can't aim for him), abom is likely to out perform. 

Unstoppable Frenzy is interchangeable for AotD. UF lines up our Unholy Frenzy with our other 2m CDs for consistent burst phases. AotD would mainly be taken for slightly more mana regen (more minion hits= more mana regen thanks to Overwhelming Force)

[rotation]
Minions
Pre-Ghouls- Abom + 2x warriors
Pre 30- Abom + 2x ghouls
2x crypt fiends works too for 3 targets or less but not a huge difference in damage

Lvl 30+
Abom + 3 Ghouls in virtually all content
2x crypt fiends + 1 ghoul is good for ST but falls behind on mana regen since 3x ghouls + all their talents

Ward - Glacial Ward (Bone Ward until you unlock it)

Mandate - Grim Mandate (Foul Mandate until you unlock it)

Opener
Rune tap right before pull>Call of the Dead> Archers> Tomb King> Plaguefather> Unholy Frenzy> Blood Fury/Berserking (if applicable) begin rotation

Rotation
Blight>Harvest Plague> spam Command Undead if enough RP/Crypt Swarm if not
Archers on CD, Blight on procs ONLY (no hardcast after pull) Lichfrost ONLY IF blight is active on target. DO NOT overwrite blights, the final tick is 25% of the full duration's damage. Rune tap as needed to spam command undead

5+ targets Rotation
Only differences here are to replace command undead with March of the Dead and spam command undead during MotD CD AND spread blight to as many targets as possible with our procs.  10s is a pretty tight window and you typically want to rune tap>MotD every time. This uses a TON of mana, even without Lich's Prodigy. You WILL be drinking/hitting Frozen Reliquary nearly every pull so spam MotD at the risk of your own bank account. 

As of writing this, the wall becomes active roughly 2-3s after casting and marches forward about 20-25 yds. Keep this in mind as these WILL AGGRO mobs so aim carefully. 

Tl;dr- maintain blight/harvest plague on at least one target, crypt swarm- mana efficient RP generator, Runetap- high mana RP generator. Spam Command Undead for free blight/lichfrost procs OR spam MotD to do massive AOE at the cost of corpse dust for Frozen Reliquaries and mana consumables

[stats]
Spell power> Intellect> Stamina.........BUT
we really want a healthy balance of Intellect/Spell power and stamina
Spell power- raw damage
Intellect- mana + crit rating
Stamina- survivability but more importantly, spell damage (15% of your minions' total stamina) yes, your stamina translates to your minions' stamina!
50+, we need to focus on hit rating as well
