[overview]
my current pyro dps raid profile which doesn't use any of the last three talent nodes because i think they are not that good

[talents]
you can swap 1 explosive point for Rapid Incineration to get more Lava Shards which results in getting more ember stacks but idk its lowkey not good

[rotation]
Get 5 ember stacks before pull

On pull cast Ignite -> Blaze -> Lava Shard 

Now if your tank is not good at keeping threat at the start just keep spamming Flare Bolt or a Meteor untill he builds up some threat . if he is good on threat start Blasting

Pop Fired Up! and Eruption and your trinkets . Start spamming Explode
keep an eye out for your ember stacks . if its getting lower than 3 stacks cast a lava Shard between Explodes to get embers . refresh Blaze if it ran out .
if you ever run out of embers while Fired Up! unlucky i guess blame rng and cast a few flare bolts to get 1 ember and back to spamming explode

After Fired Up! expire reapply all your dots and spam Flare Bolt untill you reach 5 ember stacks then spam explode until you reach 0 ember stacks . keep an eye out for Fired Up! because you need to have 5 ember stacks when it comes off cooldown.


if there are more than 1 target use Flames Of Neltharion instead of explode

[stats]
crit and haste can make your Fired Up! last longer which means more dps . so i guess


Hit Rating until cap-> Spell Power -> Crit -> Haste -> Int -> Spirit

[tips]
basically 95% of your damage comes when you have Fired Up! active so use your trinkets and Eruption Only while its active .
