[overview]
This build is great at single target, not very good at AoE but it does have a decent amount of cleave. You can move for the entire damage rotation though, so you can keep yourself safe in boss encounters or while kiting elites across the countryside in the open world.

[talents]
HH tree: Hunting for Sport, you should have near 100% uptime when you can set up and maintain your rotation. 20% armor pen is fantastic.
Barb tree: Savage Cadence and HH tree: Deep Fury are going to keep your Unbridled Rage up and available most of the time.

Barb tree: Outrage and Bloodthirsty, this is your big cooldown for a huge spike in damage. Once you get rolling and have enough crit you can keep it up for the duration of a fight.

HH tree: Gutspiller>Guts Splatter>To the Bone, this section of talents provide some great synergy that help you and your party both. Splatter gives you another source of 20% armor pen. To the Bone stacks a 20% armor sunder on the target that benefits the entire party.
HH tree: Legacy of Amani, great bonus damage when you get lucky. 
HH tree: Manhunter, you do 10% more damage vs humanoids, and everyone in your group gets 3% more crit. Quality point spent.

HH tree: Berserker and Sen'Jin's Guidance, Sen'jin's is where your rotation will finally make more sense. It will reset your Berserker Axe just like your other spears, but is not on the same cool down as your Spear abilities. Berserker is another boost (similar to Legacy of the Amani) that you won't have to worry about maintaining, it will just come naturally with your rotation.

[rotation]
Open with Unbridled Rage and Gutspiller. Gut spiller should be maintained, but not clipped because the final tick will be a Guts Spilled tick for more damage and a 4% sunder. 

Swing between burning Berserker Axe stacks or using a spear until you have to reapply Gutspiller
Use your 3 stacks of Berserker Axe. Berserker axe is going to reset after every spear while you are Enraged (should be 100%)
Javelin Toss (Sen'jin's guidance turns your throw weapon into a spear) does the most damage of the spears and is off the spear cooldown.
Headhunter's spear, decent damage, has no energy cost and bumps your energy up by 30%. Use this to keep from being energy starved. 

**the TL;DR**: Pop Outrage and macro in your Troll racial and trinkets.

**Single Target** - keep Unbridled Rage and Gutspiller rolling. Rotation starts 3 Berserker Axe > Javelin > 3 Berserker Axe > Headhunter's  spear and repeat the rotation.

For AoE - Carnage into Unbridled Rage then Spinning Axes into Gutspiller. 3 Berserker Axe > Impaling Spear > Onslaught if Carnage is down and you need to maintain energy > back to 3 berserker axe > Javelin and then repeat as best you can. You will run out of energy eventually and the real AoE classes will make you look silly anyways.

Maiming Spear is a utility for kiting and slowing down targets. 
Barbed spear is only better than Javelin if the target is being healed.

Impaling spear, decent AoE but has a wonky aiming mechanic similar to Cone of Cold or something. Make sure you are facing your target when you chuck it.

Sidenote, you can use your regular old Throw ability that everyone gets for a quick, no energy pop of damage. It works when you are standing still while spamming Berserker Axe. It is not on the same GCD as your abilities so you can just weave it in. Not much but I think its cool B)
