[overview]
A highly mobile, high burst damage, with incredibly good survivability build for the barb. This build allows you to kite most classes with ease while maintaining good dps uptime and bleeds on them, with practice you can kite multiple people as long as you have decent cooldown management.

End talents are a WIP as i constantly tweak and adjust the build, enjoy :D

[rotation]
Always start with an enrage, this build has many. Enrage procs the "You want axe?" talent that refreshes 3 charges of Berserker Axe that cost -%50 less energy.


## A good opener most of the time would be: **Unbridled Rage > Maiming Spear > 3x Berserker Axe > Gutspiller > Barbed Spear > 3x Berserker Axe** and they are usually dead. 

Enrage is also used for Ancestral Roar, one of your main regen abilities, good A.R management will allow you to stay healthy for a lot longer, especially versus other ranged classes such as Rangers and Pyros.
If you find yourself getting in trouble, pop Warband and Defiance to help mitigate some of the incoming damage, you also have Battle Vigor to enrage yourself and gain an increased 40% max HP.

Once you are out of combat, the Ancestral Keg can be used in conjunction with a lot of heals, including your Grisly Meal, allowing barbs to go from 1% hp to full in around 3-5 seconds out of combat, a very underlooked tool of their kit.

Whirling Advance is an incredible mobility tool once you get used to its jankness. Paired with Onslaught, it allows us to traverse the BG with ease, allowing us to dip in and out of combat easily. Want to be a flag carrier? Go ahead, we can keep up with most Felsworns when you get the Forward Advance talent and with good energy regen. You can also Whirling Advance into a spellcaster to Headbutt them, then spin out to start your spear/axe combos.

Then there's Gutspiller, one of the most underrated abilites a Barb has. The damage from Guts Spilled is absolutely mental as the bleed procs can, and will, crit quite often. Try to maintain the debuff on enemies as much as possible, especially when you have Twirling Axes enabled as this will spread your bleed damage over anyone in the vicinity of your target.
Speaking of bleeds, it synergizes extremely well with this build due to the higher crit chance we get with our spears from it, allowing you to have incredible burst once a target is bleeding.
