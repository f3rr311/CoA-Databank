# 🏹 Ascension Cosmic Hunter/Healer Rotation Guide 🌕

Welcome to the ultimate performance breakdown for the high-tempo **Moon Priest** build. This hybrid specialization focuses on generating **Scattered Stars** resources and detonating them via **Lunar Eclipse** windows for explosive raid healing or infinite mana sustain.

---

### 🔄 Core Single-Target & Boss Priority

The heart of this build revolves around a simple, high-velocity loop: **Build to 6 stacks, modify your state, and detonate.**

#### 1️⃣ The Stack Engine (Building Power)
* **`Huntress Shot` (Spam Filler):** Your primary baseline activity. Chain this **4 to 6 times** sequentially. 
* ⚠️ *Pro-Tip:* Resist the urge to pop your stars early! Maximizing your stacks to the cap of 6 ensures perfect global cooldown (GCD) efficiency and peak throughput.

#### 2️⃣ The Cosmic Payoff (Unleashing the Stars)
When you hit max stacks, or the raid takes a sudden chunk of damage, execute the burst engine:
* 💎 `Lunar Eclipse` ➔ 🌊 `Moonflow`
* *Why it works:* `Lunar Eclipse` completely resets and removes the cooldown of `Moonflow`. This allows you to instantly detonate your accumulated Scattered Stars for a massive tidal wave of raid healing without dropping a global.

#### 3️⃣ Tank Maintenance
* **`Touch of Moonlight`:** Your main priority is keeping *Eclipsing Arrow* active on the active tank at all times.
* *The Trick:* Always activate `Lunar Eclipse` first to turn `Touch of Moonlight` into an **instant cast**, preventing any downtime on your DPS/Healing rotation.

#### 4️⃣ The Mana Recovery Loop 🔋
If your mana pool starts dipping, immediately shift from a healing priority to a sustain priority:
* **Weave `Moon Arrow` (Rank 2):** Swap your filler from Huntress Shot to Moon Arrow. The Rank 2 variant features a hyper-short cast time, allowing you to regen mana without stalling your momentum.
* **The Mana Dump:** Instead of detonating your Scattered Stars with Moonflow, pop them using **`Lunar Lance`** to aggressively restore your mana bar.

### 💥 AOE & Trash Pull Automation

When the tank grabs massive packs of trash, your job is to turn enemy mobs into healing beacons for your party.

1.  🛡️ **Protect:** Ensure the tank is pre-buffed with *Eclipsing Arrow* (`Lunar Eclipse` ➔ `Touch of Moonlight`).
2.  🌱 **Seed:** Wait for the pack to cluster tightly, then drop **`Starshatter`**. This locks the pack down with a group stun and instantly paints **Scattered Stars** onto *every single enemy* in the pull.
3.  💥 **Detonate:** Immediately unleash **`Lunar Eclipse` ➔ `Moonflow`**. Detonating Scattered Stars across a massive clump of enemies simultaneously produces an unparalleled amount of localized AOE healing.
