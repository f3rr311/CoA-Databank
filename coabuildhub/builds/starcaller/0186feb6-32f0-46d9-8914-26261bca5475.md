**Introduction**
If you are going Warden, you already know that you aren't here to run into 10v10 as a frontline tank, nor are you spamming aoe spells in range like many casters.
In fact, other classes will generally underestimate you because you deal almost no damage during the initial buildup phase and you are so squishy that you can get two-shotted by many other DPS classes when caught out of position.
But that is to your advantage when you learn to play right.
Wardens need to weave range and melee seamlessly as if playing hunter and warrior simulataneously in classic WoW.

Your primary roles and effectiveness are:
- Chasing down targets
- Fighting small skirmishes (1v1, 2v2, 3v3)
- Stalling as you run around and forcing people to chase you while you soften them up before going in melee range

As such, in big scale battles, just play a typical range building Scattered Stars and spamming Lunar Lance (LL).
Look for opportunities to go in for a quick kill then get out again.
If pulled in against a crowd, just immediately get out; don't even try to fight - run away while getting a few hits in.

In 1v1 or 2v2 you shine the brightest.
You won't simply one shot people like some of the other classes and your time to kill will be higher in general.
But what you lack in upfront damage, you make up with a plethora of movement and survival spells that, if played right, allows you to pretty much fight against any class in the game.
Don't get greedy, don't forget you are not really tanky despite wearing plate, and always try to create space when your AB is on cd.
You've got a whopping 45 range... USE IT


**Basic strat**
Engage in range to build up Scattered Stars.
At around 6-7 Star stacks, get in closer (either voluntarily or forced) to use Sentinel Glaive + Fan of Knives (if you are lvl 57+) + AB.
Spam LL 3-4 times continuously as the stacks are being consumed (if you didn't know, yes LL can be used multiple times after the first trigger - as long as there is still a Star stack on the oppoent).
Your auto attacks while using LL may give you another one or two Star stacks, which allows you get more LL off.
Use movement abilities to create space again and rinse and repeat.
Wardens are very squishy despite wearing plates, so don't fight toe-to-toe against real melee units for long.


**Combat tips**
- When in range of 20 yrds, spam Sentinel Glaive; should be used as soon as its off cd.
- Don't forget to use Lunar Eclipse/Moonwell Splash as soon as you lose a chunk of health.
You need the Lunar Eclipse stacks to keep building back up as you will need them to fight.
Treat it like another dps rotation; very high priority if you've lost enough health to use it.
- Avatar of Vengeance will make the rotation is easier, but against real melee units you will still be very squishy, so get out if your Astral Blade is on cd.
- Use Celestial Aegis while fighting in melee, especially if you have a high stack of Stars since they will give you more than enough mana back.
- Aspect of the Cosmos is used in most situations in order to stack Stars the fastest, but with the 5 sec cd, you can switch aspects as needed.
- Don't forget about the Command spell; great against hard casters like Stormbringer by forcing them to move.
- If you are OOM alot, you are probably not using your LL enough.
Always use it if you feel like your target is about to go out of range; you need that mana always topped up.
- If you are using NE (are you not role-playing as PotM???), don't forget about using Shadowmeld strategically both in and out of combat.
I love to use it on top of the mining entrance in Arathi; you have enough range to hit anyone targeting the banner if you stand right at the tip.
In combat it can be used to throw people off their rotation, especially if you are not dotted.


**Other tips**
- Always run the pvp trinket to break cc in addition to the Moonwell Water spell.
- Even though Intelligence is important for damage, never sacrifice your health for it.
Stamina is still king of pvp, so try to build a balanced build between Stamina and Intelligence (and Str if incidental) as that is often the most efficient stat build when it comes to equipment.
- Engineering will take your pvp to the next level but thats sweat tier.
