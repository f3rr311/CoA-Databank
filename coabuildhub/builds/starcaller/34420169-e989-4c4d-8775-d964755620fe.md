[overview]
This is what I have been using while leveling in dungeons. Hope you find it useful!

[rotation]
You will always use Aspect of the Cosmos for it´s insane Scattered Star generation. 

Your basic AoE rotation is to open with Starburst into Starsweep. At 30+ you should be able to pull pretty decent pack sizes of 10+ mobs then you can just go rotate between these 2 spells. However if there are smaller packs or you or your group is squishy. You might wanna cleave in between to make sure to have generated enough Scattered Stars to reset your Starsweep.

ST  prio Moonblade when it procs otherwise just spam Starsunder into Lunar Lance at max stars. 

For survivability top yourself with Lunar Eclipse into Touch of Moonlight. 

Blanket of Stars and Infused Aegis into big melee packs for added damage and 10% dmg amp from Starlight Duelist talent.
