[overview]
I am using this while leveling.  It has been effective in PvE, PvP, and open world PvP/leveling.  Only level 36 but enemies die in 1-3 hits.  Need to juggle vows for mana regeneration but mana is not really an issue.

Always tops charts in aoe dungeon pulls, while still performing well in ST.  

In PvP can quickly delete backline squishies if you can avoid getting CCed to death.

Probably not the best and will adjust as I continue to level, but so far this has been extremely viable.
