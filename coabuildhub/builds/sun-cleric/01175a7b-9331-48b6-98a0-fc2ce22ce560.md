This build is engineered primarily for Mythic+ (M+) performance. While it functions well during leveling, players must mind dungeon crowd-control (CC) profiles (such as STUNlet Monastery).

Adjustments are required for pure Single-Target (ST) content, though the fundamental playstyle remains mostly intact without significant damage loss.

[talents]
Depending on the encounter type, choose between Divine Retribution and Knight of Dawn:

Divine Retribution: Simulates significantly higher in AoE/Cleave scenarios.

Knight of Dawn: Performs exceptionally well if played correctly, though less consistent in large pulls.

⚠️ Important Talent Update (07/26/26): Swap Judgement of the Valkyr for Trial of Valor!

[rotation]
**Pre-Pull Management**
Always attempt to stack 20 Solar Power (SP) prior to engaging a pull:
In Raids or long runs: Spam Solar Invocation: Conquest (SIC) until you reach 20 SP.
If starting at 0 SP: Spam SIC directly into the pull sequence.

💥 Burst Sequences by Encounter Type

(2 – 4 Targets)
🔵 Vow: **Vow of Dawn**

Dawn -> Paragon -> Dawnfall -> Champion of the Sun + Radiance -> Divine Retribution -> Judgement Day (Main Burst Window) -> Sunslam (Your highest damage ability) -> Horusath Blast ->  Glorious Execution -> Execute PRIO LIST (2-4) for two cycles -> Radiant Conversion (Manually turn OFF Dawn) -> Rebuild Phase: Generate Solar Power and maintain with SIC.

Priority List (2-4 Targets): **Horusath Blast** > **Glorious Execution** > **Gavel of Grace** > **Justice**

(6+ Targets)
🔵 Vow: **Vow of Dawn** 

Dawn -> Paragon -> Dawnfall -> Champion of the Sun + Radiance -> Divine Retribution -> Judgement Day -> Sunslam -> Valkyr's Calling + Horusath Blast -> Glorious Execution -> Execute PRIO LIST (6+) -> Radiant Conversion -> SIC -> Rebuild Solar Power

Priority List (6+ Targets): **Horusath Blast** > **Justice** > **Glorious Execution** > **Gavel of Grace**
(If Knight of Dawn is choose, switch the priority of **Glorious Execution** and **Justice**).

(Single Target)
🔴 Vow: **Vow of the Valkyr** 

Dawn -> Paragon -> Dawnfall -> Champion of the Sun + Radiance -> Divine Retribution -> Sunslam -> Horusath Blast -> Glorious Execution -> Execute PRIO LIST (ST) -> Radiant Conversion -> SIC -> PRIO LIST (ST) -> Dawn -> PRIO LIST (ST) -> Valkyr's Calling + Horusath Blast -> Rebuild Solar Power with SIC.

Priority List (Single Target): **Horusath Blast** > **Glorious Execution** > **Gavel of Grace** > **Justice**

💡 **Note on Valkyr's Calling**: Has lower priority on single-target encounters since its damage is flat rather than multiplicative. Maintain rotation flow rather than delaying main abilities.

**General Resource & Stack Loop**
Managing Stacks: Avoid dropping all SP at once. Aim to drop between 8–10 Dawn stacks (after entering Dawn phase) before manually turning it off.

If SIC is off CD at 10 stacks: Turn off Dawn and rebuild immediately using SIC.
If SIC is on CD: Drop down to at least 7 stacks and rebuild using normal generators until SIC comes off cooldown to push back to 20 SP.

**Optional Tech**: Use of the New DayFor an aggressive playstyle, trigger Use of the New Day right after entering the rebuild phase. This allows an immediate re-burst. However, overall DPS flow suffers since the resets are flat and do not align smoothly with main rotational cooldowns.
**Mana Caution**: The full burst execution burns through Mana very rapidly. Keep Mana management in mind on long fights!

[stats]
**Primary Stats**
Strength > Agility > Intellect (I was wrong by not noticing Agility power here)

**Secondary Stat Priority**
**Haste (3º Softcap)** > **Attack Power** > **Crit (25\% base / 10\% min)** > **Spell Power** > **Armor Pen**

Haste: Reaching the 3rd softcap is optimal long-term, but avoid over-prioritizing bad gear pieces early on just for Haste gains.
For who donnot know what is softcap, is the cap that you overload a GCD while casting or Melee-Swing Damage, take note that you gain haste activating your Dawn of 6sec.
The Haste Softcaps is % based. 5%, 12%, 16%, 20%, 25%. Now you know what's the softcaps based on wotlk.
For 50% haste (reducing 1.5-second base casts/GCD to 1.0 second) is the max cap. Our cap AT THE BEGINNING IS 16% so.

Critical Strike: Aim for ~25% overall. The hard mechanical minimum threshold is 10%.
Spell Power: Has lower interaction scaling than expected—do not stack intentionally.
5MPS & Tank Stats: Unnecessary for this DPS spec.

Hard Caps: Hit Rating8% Expertise 26 (They are a must)

[tips]
The major challenge with this build is managing mismatched cooldown timers (45s, 1m, 1.5m (x2 45s), 2m, 3m, 5m, 10m).

Recommended Realignment Strategy:
Treat 45s abilities as 1-minute CDs: Hold your 45s CDs to pair directly with Divine Retribution (1m).

Convert to a 1m / 2m class profile: Aligning major windows prevents wasted uptime and awkward downtime gaps.

Hold 5m / 10m CDs for your major 2-minute burst windows.

**Avoid It's Always Sunny Talent**: Testing shows this talent degrades gameplay quality, drops overall m+ DPS by desynchronization, and fails to fix alignment issues. On another scenarios i couldn't and can't test properly, check out Darkguardian guide for it.
