[overview]
Stance Dancing allows you to keep up with most DPS - especially after you add in Blessing of Absolution to your own dps !

This is a faster paced playstyle if you want to do as much dps as possible while providing your groups with a ton of healing.

[talents]
Change out Sanctified, and Faith for Everglow, and Sunbeams for Raids.

I don't think Cultivate Divinity + Empowered Holy Form is worth it because you'll probably not be casting Illumination a lot.

With more Haste - Gateway to Heaven becomes more attractive than Blazing Chariot - then your whole goal is to get in and out of Dawn as fast as possible. Though if you prefer a slightly slower playstyle then Blazing Chariot is still great !

You can take Unbreakable Focus, Suncharged (it does ~5% of your damage in dungeons), or Gleaming Vigil. 
I'm going with Unbreakable Focus combined with Final Vows, and Heaven's Appraisal it will allow you to get back into Vow of Dawn spam quicker. 

Enveloped in Light is a PvP talent since you're not going to be wearing much Mail and Plate at 60.

[rotation]
You'll want to be stance dancing as much as possible ! 

Solar Invocation: Conquest should be used on cooldown, and I think it's optimal to Radiant Conversion every time you're below ~85% mana. 

The rotation should be something like: 

Vow of Grace to build Solar Power -> Vow of Dawn + Dawn -> Sunflare / Heals -> Radiant Conversion + Conquest -> Vow of Grace -> Repeat

Your goal isn't to use all your stacks, but to maintain your mana as much as possible to keep spamming Sunflares inside of Vow of Dawn. Stance dancing, and using Radiant Conversion early with Conquest will allow you to get back into Dawn quickly. 

You rarely ever want to be building your own Solar Power, and that's why you always want to rely on Vow of Grace.

Vow of Grace and Vow of Dawn are the bread and butter, while Vow of Radiance should be used for the Dawn effect situationally - boss damage when you're not regenning, or to pump out massive heals when pulls go wrong.

Once you've picked up Dawnfall you can swap to Vow of Grace to get full mana every time you use Dawnfall. You can even wait for the last ~5 seconds, or as mobs are beginning to die to swap to Grace. 

Stand in melee to get as much Gavel of Grace, Judgement Day, and Radiance off as possible.

-

Once you get higher haste or two points into Guiding Glow - 

You can begin to cycle through Dawns very quickly for Gateway to Heaven: 

Start in Vow of Grace → Vow of Dawn + Dawn → 3-6 casts → Conquest + Radiant Conversion → 1-2 casts → Dawn again → 3-6 casts -> Radiant Conversion + Vow of Grace for 3-5 seconds -> Vow of Dawn + Dawn -> Repeat

When you have Conquest off cooldown stay in Vow of Dawn and Radiant Conversion at 10 or 9 stacks, or when Radiance ends - whichever happens first. 

It should be optimal to drop Dawn at any time around 10-8 stacks.

[macros]
I've been using this for Blessing of Absolution so I don't have to mouseover the tank every time, it's definitely increased my DPS a lot since doing so:

#showtooltip
/cast [@mouseover, help][help][@player] Bless
/focus [@mouseover, help][help][@player]

#showtooltip
/cast [@focus] Blessing of Absolution

#showtooltip
/cast [@focus] Blessing of Purity

If you don't want the Focus Target on your screen - with Move Anything make sure to make the frame smaller, then drag it into the top corner because the frame will jump scare you sometimes. 

There's a weird bug with the Focus Target macro or frame where you won't be able to cast Blessing of Absolution again until you recast Bless on the Tank. This seems to only happen when you try to refresh Bless during combat.

Conquest Macro:

#showtooltip Solar Invocation: Conquest
/cast Radiant Conversion
/cast Solar Invocation: Conquest

This will end your Dawn, and let you cast Conquest at the same time since Dawn isn't on the GCD. There's been many times where I've accidentally hit Conquest while I was still inside of Dawn, and this macro should fix that !

Stance Macros: 

#showtooltip Vow of Radiance
/cast Vow of Radiance
/cast Dawn

#showtooltip Vow of Dawn
/cast Vow of Dawn
/cast Dawn

#showtooltip Vow of Grace

These will 100% get messy if you're not careful. You should still have Dawn on a comfortable bind on your action bars.

[tips]
During dungeons it's not uncommon for you to go full Dps -> Regen -> Dps -> Regen - some packs you'll only get to do 400 damage while you're spamming your single target Sunflare in Vow of Grace, and that's okay ! You don't want your group to stop for you. 

You also won't always have the best groups to play in, so in those it's safer to stay inside of Vow of Grace, while occasionally finding windows to pop into Vow of Dawn. At minimum you should be swapping stances to use Horusath Blast as an AoE.
