In our main build, we'll focus on mobility and endurance, as well as runic regeneration. We'll utilize several key cooldowns to maximize our survivability and damage.

1: Tormented Soul
2:Sytche Rush
3: Wraithblade
4: Vellwalk for speed
5: Soultap for Fast Runic
6: Limbo for Autoclean
7: Jailer Shield

Domination: 
Here in the Domination branch, we have a variation: some people play with Guardian and rely on its shield, but honestly, it has a pretty long cooldown (3 minutes) and the shield is mediocre. It’s better to have the damage mitigation from Life Harness and the constant reset from our Dreadwake.

[rotation]
For single-target combat, we’ll rely solely on Reap; we’ll charge our souls and use Tormented Souls—this will be our primary defensive ability. Then we’ll proceed to spam Dreadwake and Soul Strike. In addition, if we aren’t taking too much damage, we can use the Scythe and Relic of the Lost to maximize damage.

It’s also very important to remember that during damage spikes, we’ll need to use Bolstered Form, Soul Strike, and Tormented Soul to survive. In addition, we can support ourselves by using Deathwing, as it will help us heal 10% more from all sources.

Big Pull Dungeons:
Here we’ll change our strategy. We’ll aim for a large pull, since the larger the pull, the more healing, resilience, and runic regeneration we’ll have. It’s very important that we always charge our Tormented Soul to the maximum before starting a pull to ensure safety. We’ll begin the pull with Sytche Rush, then return to a position to cast LOS with Vellwalk. (Spells are our worst enemy.) Then, once the pull is grouped together, we’ll use Soul Tap and cast Dreadwake until we have Tormented Souls. With our Tormented Souls buff, we’ll use 3 Dreadwakes to fully charge our souls, and then we’ll use Relic of the Lost. then we’ll use Reap and apply our Wraithblade to one target.
