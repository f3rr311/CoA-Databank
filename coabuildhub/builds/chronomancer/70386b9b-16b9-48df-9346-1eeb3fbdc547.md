This is the best build for healing Mythic content for now. After good testing, Chronomancer is still solid in Mythic content, especially in big pulls where you benefit the most from your CDs.

## **UPDATE** 
You can swap **Infinite** + **Infinite Shield** for **Gravity Bomb** + **Chaos Omen** for Mythic 0 and Mythic + play if you feel confortable without the spell so it add an extra big packing to your group every minutes !

We changed some talent in the healer build, we now play around **Fortify Timeline** and **Correct the Mistake** for big aoe healing since we don't have issue on singletarget. You will have to play around your **Aeon swap** to benefit from **Through The Aeons** big mana regen.

[rotation]
**PrePull**
- **Aeon of Oblivion**
- **Accelerated Recorvery** on all your party
- **Epoch** to apply **Oblivion** buff (3% damage buff on your party)
**During Fight**
- **Aeon of Resilience** if it's a scary fight, otherwise you can stay in oblivion for big damage
- Ripple big dam if you stayed in **Aeon of Oblivion**
- Keep **AR** up atleast on tank
- Use **Correct the Mistake** if your party health is going low and **Ripple** is not available

**CDs**
- **Infinite Shield** on tank (can do around 20% of your HPS if you manage it nicely but dont overdo and lose all your mana to keep his uptime to 100%)
- **Timeguard** on tank
- **Hasten** during critical time or if you want to burst damage
- **Temporal Anomaly** if you are taking damage constantly in the next 10 sec
- ** Desynchronization** very good against scary melee/ranged monsters and works on boss too
- ** Infinite Clone/Rewind** use it if you are going to spend a lot of mana in the next 10 sec (press it high on mana and don't forget to use **Rewind** before the buff end)

[stats]
**Spirit** is your prio. You can take some **Crit** to stack **Cadence of Time** but it's not game changer.
