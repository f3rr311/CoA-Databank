[overview]
Time spec is **NOT** easy. Like at all. You need to invest some **time** *(heh..)* to master the art of stance swapping. Outside of stance swapping you still need to master the external buffs you give.


## Overview

This spec is built around uptiming your HoTs and buffs on your group and dealing moderate AoE damage with the help of stance swapping every 5 seconds. 

Time brings several unique buffs and spells in it's kit to your m+ run:
[Aeon of Oblivion](coa-skill:chronomancer:806293) - Permanent 3% damage buff on your group
[Aeon of Protection](coa-skill:chronomancer:806292) - Permanent 5% damage reduction on your group
[Continuum Restoration](coa-skill:chronomancer:801271) - Mass dispell
[Hasten](coa-skill:chronomancer:801304) - Power Infusion for your dps (if [Unmaker of Realities](coa-skill:chronomancer:706107) is taken) or both for you and your dps (if you take Maker of Realities)
[Resynchronize](coa-skill:chronomancer:804495) - Mass res if your group wiped or you can res yourself while in combat to consume battle res charge.

## Raid buffs
48 spirit or 37 intellect.

## Survivability 
[Temporal Anomaly](coa-skill:chronomancer:806315) is an insane defensive to keep you alive.
[Clasp of Infinity](coa-skill:chronomancer:805847) and [Waves of Time](coa-skill:chronomancer:801277) should be more than enough to keep enemies far from you while your tank is gathering your pull.

## Damage output
Most of your damage output will be [Aeon of Oblivion](coa-skill:chronomancer:806293) + [Ripple](coa-skill:chronomancer:806296). This combo does moderate damage because of your passive level 50 capstone: [Eternity Warper](coa-skill:chronomancer:806301). 
In single target encounters in addition to dps ripple casts, uptime your Decomposition debuff. [Unmake](coa-skill:chronomancer:804418) if not mana starved.

## F.A.Q
### Why no **Improved Reverse Wound** *(class row 2)*?
- There's no much point in it, because you are overwhelmed in your [Endless Sands](coa-skill:chronomancer:806338) procs, becasue of [Wonders of Time](coa-skill:chronomancer:560541) procs and your manual [Epoch](coa-skill:chronomancer:801275) casts. It take you only 2 stacks of [Endless Sands](coa-skill:chronomancer:806338) ative for your [Reverse Wound](coa-skill:chronomancer:801303) to be in your GCD range. Mostly you always have instacast stored anyways.

### Why **Unearthed Pools** *(class row 2)*?
- Even if we are not casting [Unmake](coa-skill:chronomancer:804418) that much we still get plenty of mana discount for **most** of our spells, even [Ripple](coa-skill:chronomancer:806296).

### Why no [Temporal Focus](coa-skill:chronomancer:806165) *(class row 6)*
- It is highly situational button to press, mostly useless. Can take it in specific dungeons, unlearn **Timewalking** *(class row 8)* in exchange.

### [Hasten](coa-skill:chronomancer:801304): Maker or Unmaker *(class row 7)*
- You always Hasten your DPS. **ALWAYS**. You just decide do you need hasten aswel *(you get 20% haste back if you target your ally)* or you go full-damage external. Never [Hasten](coa-skill:chronomancer:801304) yourself, you will drain fast and there is no circumstance when you need it.

### [Infinite Shield](coa-skill:chronomancer:520457) vs [Gravity Bomb](coa-skill:chronomancer:501820)
- [Infinite Shield](coa-skill:chronomancer:520457) does insane ammount of your overall healing keeping tank alive at all times. Since tank is your 85% overall healing done to its impossible to drop this talent in exchange to [Gravity Bomb](coa-skill:chronomancer:501820) juicy mass grip mechanic. Higher key level higher damage your tank will take.
*Most of the time you will LoS big packs anyways so they will stick in one place anyways.*

### [Epic Recovery](coa-skill:chronomancer:706098)?
- Yes. This talent is your game-changer. Even if it casts only Rank 1 [Epoch](coa-skill:chronomancer:801275) it still applies buffs from our [Eternity Warper](coa-skill:chronomancer:806301). More info in **Rotation** segment.

### Why no capstones in spec tree?
- I tried to play with them. Honestly. But all these talents either redundant or useless in general, even though they are fun in tooltips. [Buy Time](coa-skill:chronomancer:801280) is a huge disappointment. The only potential take is **Converge the Infinite** but there are so little highly AoE encounters in m+ for it to be vailable. [The Vast Infinite](coa-skill:chronomancer:706083) is a good capstone, but its gated with useless talent. Could be taken if it was swapped places with [Buy Time](coa-skill:chronomancer:801280).

### Why no [Fabric of Time](coa-skill:chronomancer:806299)?
- You almost never want or need to swap faster than aeon's regular 5 second cd. Your **Through the Aeons** forces you to swap every 8 seconds only. Your [Epoch](coa-skill:chronomancer:801275) base healing is not as impacted as you think for you to be starved in aeon swapping.

### Possible swap-overs:
- [Temporal Focus](coa-skill:chronomancer:806165) vs **Timewalking**
- **Displacement** vs **Ideal Time**
- [Continuum Restoration](coa-skill:chronomancer:801271) vs **Displacement**
*You can pretty much rotate **Displacement**, **Ideal Time**, [Continuum Restoration](coa-skill:chronomancer:801271)* between each other.

[rotation]
## Core
- Uptime your **Through the Aeons** buff via stance swapping.
- Uptime your [Infinite Shield](coa-skill:chronomancer:520457) and **Timeguard** on your tank.
- [Aeon of Renewal](coa-skill:chronomancer:806290) + [Epoch](coa-skill:chronomancer:801275) cast apply Renewal HoT, try to uptime it on the tank as high as possible.
- **Temporal Restoration** buff should be active for additional mana regen.

### Main 
- Keep [Accelerated Recovery](coa-skill:chronomancer:800857) active on your group at all times.
- Extend it with [Fortify Timeline](coa-skill:chronomancer:804491).
- Swap to [Aeon of Resilience](coa-skill:chronomancer:806291) to keep 5% damage reduction buff on your group via [Epic Recovery](coa-skill:chronomancer:706098).
- Swap to [Aeon of Oblivion](coa-skill:chronomancer:806293) to keep 3% damage done buff on your group via [Epic Recovery](coa-skill:chronomancer:706098). Cast [Ripple](coa-skill:chronomancer:806296) afterwards if group is not in danger. It will do tonn of damage, because of [Eternity Warper](coa-skill:chronomancer:806301).
- Swap to [Aeon of Protection](coa-skill:chronomancer:806292) for regular [Epoch](coa-skill:chronomancer:801275) single target healing.
*Additional options:*
- [Reverse Wound](coa-skill:chronomancer:801303) with 5 stacks of [Endless Sands](coa-skill:chronomancer:806338) for emergency or tank healing.
-  If you are experiencing high-aoe ticking damage on your group you can apply renewal hot from your [Aeon of Renewal](coa-skill:chronomancer:806290)+[Epoch](coa-skill:chronomancer:801275) on your group targetly 1 by 1, because [Epic Recovery](coa-skill:chronomancer:706098) applies only rank 1 renewal, which is bad. 
- Cast [Infinite Clone](coa-skill:chronomancer:804492) before heavy healing and use [Rewind](coa-skill:chronomancer:801294) afterwards to restore some of the spent mana.

Most of the times it is way more than enough to keep your group alive but if you are in heavy-aoe encounter. You can add some [Correct the Mistake](coa-skill:chronomancer:572352) casts and [Aeon of Renewal](coa-skill:chronomancer:806290) + [Ripple](coa-skill:chronomancer:806296).

### Ripple + [Eternity Warper](coa-skill:chronomancer:806301)
- [Aeon of Renewal](coa-skill:chronomancer:806290) - Good choice if you encounter ticking damage or middle-heavy aoe damage.
- [Aeon of Protection](coa-skill:chronomancer:806292) - Should be exactly timed for specific mechanic. Highly situational.
- [Aeon of Oblivion](coa-skill:chronomancer:806293) - Your main source of damage and the most impactful cast of [Ripple](coa-skill:chronomancer:806296). You do some damage while channeling and heal afterwards for high ammount if your group suffered some damage while channeling.
- [Aeon of Resilience](coa-skill:chronomancer:806291) - please don't...

[leveling]
## Talent rush:
- Try to get **Through the Aeons** as soon as possible. Mana is always a problem on low levels until you get this talent.
- Your next step is rushing to **Chronicler**. This will make a lot easier for you to uptime the [Accelerated Recovery](coa-skill:chronomancer:800857) on your group.


## Aeons swap:
- The only 2 aeons you need while leveling are: [Aeon of Renewal](coa-skill:chronomancer:806290) and [Aeon of Protection](coa-skill:chronomancer:806292). 
Uptime your Renewal HoT from [Epoch](coa-skill:chronomancer:801275) on tank and heal others with [Aeon of Protection](coa-skill:chronomancer:806292).
After level 50 when you get [Eternity Warper](coa-skill:chronomancer:806301) passive, cast [Ripple](coa-skill:chronomancer:806296) with [Aeon of Renewal](coa-skill:chronomancer:806290) active.
