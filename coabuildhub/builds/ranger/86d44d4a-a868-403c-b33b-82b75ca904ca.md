[overview]
Build is focused on doing high damage numbers with Incendiary Shot on single target, even if Incendiary Shot hits AOE. 
For bigger numbers use Searing Quiver and Oil Dip on ranged weapon.

Alternative option is using Poison Quiver with Oil Dip on mainhand and Burning Toxin on ranged weapon.

Note: Don't use Incendiary Shot if Oil Dip dot is not on target.
