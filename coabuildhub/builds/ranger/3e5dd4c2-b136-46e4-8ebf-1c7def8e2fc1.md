[overview]
Ranger Archery is first and foremost NOT a replacement of Hunter. So if you are looking for the hunter playstyle you wont find it here.

This usually gets me to 4-7k dps on ST and much more in AoE.. It does however, vary. Theres a lot of positioning to be aware of at all times.

[talents]
The most impactful talents is by *FAR* [Incendiary Shot](coa-skill:ranger:524870) It not only adds damage in AoE but also ST rotations so it stays no matter what. I have yet to try out 'Headshots only' for increased precision shot damage however.

[rotation]
Keep [Skirmish](coa-skill:ranger:802039) and [Serrated Shot](coa-skill:ranger:500073) Up at ALL Times.

Spend only on 5 Advantage points.

Cast [Incendiary Shot](coa-skill:ranger:524870) on CD
Cast [Precision Shot](coa-skill:ranger:500075)
Cast [Deadshot](coa-skill:ranger:520227) 
Cast [Skullpiercer](coa-skill:ranger:802036) ONLY if Skirmish is about to run out, or you cannot get in minimum range for a Precision Shot

ST: [Quick Shot](coa-skill:ranger:500074)

AoE: [Hunting Shot](coa-skill:ranger:801191)


Quiver: [Skirmisher's Quiver](coa-skill:ranger:801069)

[leveling]
Personally, I recommend Dungeon spam. Dont bother trying to level with this in the open world.

But if you are dead-set on leveling, Either you take Farstrider as it seems to do a lot better than this spec in low levels. Or you can always drop a point in [Resourceful] to lower the CD of Snapseed & Crippling shot to keep distance. You will not be able to zoom-tank major groups as easily but with some range management, not impossible. I also recommend Toxic / Searing Quiver for leveling. The same rule of thumb applies to rotation to keep Skirmish up and try to refresh it with full advantage spenders. Another great talent pick is Forest Dweller in higher levels to keep yourself in action longer.
