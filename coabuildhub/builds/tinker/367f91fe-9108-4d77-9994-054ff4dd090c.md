[overview]
From my playtime so far enjoying the demolition build and testing different variants for the current state I think this is the best talent setup we can run with at the moment. Once SBF works we can swap to that for maybe both aoe but definatelly for ST + taking Tank Buster to boost our ST even more.

[talents]
Since SBF doesn't work properly for current content I think this is the best setup. If SBF will work when raid is out I would go for that instead of HBB + Tank Buster.

[rotation]
Most of our playstyle revolves on pressing the CD's as soon as avaliable.

In all situations we start with Build Sentry Turret as it duplicates all our rockets and has special attacks on our sticky (SB) bomb and bomb toss ( BT ) and also when avaliable put Oil Spill-Pylon for increased damage + Air Strike .

Single Target Rotation : 
Piercing Aug 
Sticky Bomb > Spider BOMB + Deathball > Bomb Toss > Firepot Drone > Scrap Shot > Firepot Drone > Scrap Shot > ( Rocket Launcher when proc'd ) > HBB (after all bomb's explode detonate / usually i wait for 2nd sticky bomb ) 

AoE Rotation : 
Explosive Aug
Sticky Bomb > Spider Bomb + Deathball > Bomb Toss > Firepot Done > HBB > Scrap Shot ( RL on proc + Rockadier at this cast ) > Firepot Drone > Scrap Shot > Detonate 



Honorable mention for why the rotation looks like this is the fact that  Firepot Drone, Deathball and Spider Bomb are not affected by GCD so it's the best if we can stack those casts in between GCD's for maximum efficiency.

PS : Always wait 1 second for Bomb Toss before you use Spider Bomb, that way you get 3 Bomb Toss instead of 1 .

[stats]
Agility > Attack Power = Spell damage > Critical Strike > Intellect
