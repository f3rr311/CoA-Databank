[overview]
**Help your group overcome challenges with your Nanobots, Beacons, Explosions and Zap!**
*Don't be a healbot*

Always rotate your Augment, use explosive on 2+ targets, piercing during single target fights and stim when your group requires more green numbers from you. Keep your Nanobot Reconstruction and Nanobot Swarm on tank, mana regenerating bots on self and refresh all each minute. Position yourself a few yards from the tank, so the whole group benefits from your beacons. Keep one Replenishment Beacon up at all times. If you know there will be group damage incoming, you can prepare the group with shielding beacon and overcharge it for a group wide shield.

**PET**
Remember to put your Sentry Turret when the fight starts (beware it auto shots your target, so don't do it too early if you have the boss/trash targetted). You can cast Sentry Turret while casting something else.
Use ZIGGI-6K (your signature pet) at all times. It does a lot of damage compared to other pets and passively heals with that damage. Even when you're running solo, it's not worth to use the bear, instead, throw your misdirect nanobots (Swarm) on your Sentry Turret and let it tank for you, once it's down just put a new one and repeat.
If your pet dies or decides to go on its own journey somewhere, you can resummon it instantly after using Basic Intuition.
Watch your pet's mana from time to time, if it's getting low, pop its recharge ability, preferably during downtime. If you find yourself forgetting about pet's mana recharge, macro it with Air Strike, it's less optimal, but you can forget about that chore.
Don't use your pet's Revitalizing Aura, the healing provided is not worth the mana used.

**DPS**
For aoe don't waste your mana on Scrap Shot, but use Sticky Bomb on cooldown and make sure you have your explosive augment on for aoe auto shots. Pop an airstrike when you know the fight will be long enough. Use battle turret for big pulls if you know the group can stay alive while you're in it or have someone else control it.
For single target use piercing augment, throw a Sticky Bomb on cooldown and spam Scrap Shots as long as mana is not an issue.
Remember that you don't auto shoot while moving, so try to find a comfortable place and stay there. If you need to reposition yourself, do a few steps and stop for a shot, distance depends on the attack speed of your weapon, you'll get to feel it.

**Healing**
As previously mentioned, keep your nanobots on the tank, they will heal the whole group when they are in range. Zapping someone with nanobots is a guaranteed crit that leads to more aoe healing. Always prioritize Zap! for direct healing, if the tank takes heavy constant damage throw them a Med Pack (remember it has a pretty short range, so you might want to position yourself close enough to the tank, but far enough from the enemies to shoot them). Use beacons and overcharge them whenever needed (more details in Rotation). If somehow the group requires more healing, or someone is spread too much and stays out of range of beacons or nanobots (you can pull them with pet's active ability - see macros), shoot em with Repair Shot. For healing heavy fights swap to Stim Augmentation over explosive/piercing one.

**Resources**
Keep mana nanobots on yourself at all times and position yourself in replenishment beacon. Overcharge in a single Replenishment Beacon might not give you much mana (based on your total, if recharge costs more than 10% of your mana, it's even gonna drain you), so try to overcharge 2 or more whenever you can.
You should be fine without Mana Module, so use Power Module on yourself - it increases your dps by a lot. *Don't be a healbot*

**Crowd Control**
Your main two sources of important CC are
- Macro Gravity Zone - 2 min cooldown aoe slow leading to a 6s stun. Very strong on big pulls, it's also always good to use it after calling an air strike, so the mobs don't leave the zone before it crashes.
- Noise Box - 1.5 min cooldown aoe interrupt, it's close range unfortunately, but if you see a group of casters far away and the group doesn't LoS them, use your Rocket Boots and interrupt them.

All other tools are very situational. 
Discombobulate! can be used to interupt an important cast, but it won't prevent from casting after the cc ends and even if you're the only person targetting that enemy, your turret will l likely shoot them and end the disorient, unless you macro a mouseover.
I strongly suggest not using Deploy Blast Mine in a stack of mobs, as it knocks back both them and your group, messing up what the tank has prepared. The only good use of it is to bring stray enemies back to the stack, but the preperation part makes it usually not worth it.

[talents]
If you notice that you rarely use an Air Strike, try using it, really. If you still don't and have that one player in your group (or yourself) going down all the time, put 2 last points of Tinker to the right side of the tree.

[rotation]
If the tank stops to gather a pack or engages a boss, pop an air strike, throw sticky bomb, put down your sentry turret and start placing beacons. Remember that all beacons except Shield can stack, both their passive and overcharge effect, so never stack two shielding ones.

- Shield Beacon - start with it, in an emergency situation your overcharge will apply a shield
- Replenishment Beacon - always aim to have at least one up (you can have more than 1, they will stack, both passively and after an overcharge)
- Restorative Beacon - only if the group takes constant damage higher than your nanobots output, or your Zap! is far from getting off cooldown and you know you will need more aoe healing. If that's not the case, it's better to hold 1 beacon stack off cooldown for Alarm Beacon, or if it's not needed - throw down another Replenishment Beacon.

If the pull looks scary, pop your Macro-Gravity Zone on tank, it will slow the mobs and lead to an aoe stun.
If there are lots of casters, or any of the mobs has an important cast (eg. Hex), cast your Noise Box.
If you notice someone gets Charm, Fear or Sleep, or you know enemies are going to throw those at you, pop an Alarm Beacon.

[stats]
Always prioritize intellect as it boosts your ranged attack power, healing power, crit strike rating and mana (the more mana you have the more you regenerate with beacons!)

Intellect > Spellpower > Crit Chance > Haste > MP5

[gear]
While leveling, don't force yourself into using mail armor only. If a cloth with more intellect drops, be happy about it!
Treat your melee weapon as a stat stick for int and try to pair it with a shield for more survivability.
The attack speed on your gun matters the most, as you proc your agumentations with each shot. My suggestion would be [Precisely Calibrated Boomstick](https://db.ascension.gg/?item=2100)

[leveling]
On low levels mana is your biggest problem. Overcharge might cost more than it returns with a single beacon. Until level 40 use Clockwork Pet for 20% mana back every minute, then Ziggi comes shining with your Graftbolts passive, but if you keep getting mana starved, feel free to keep Clockwork until you notice you don't need its extra mana.
