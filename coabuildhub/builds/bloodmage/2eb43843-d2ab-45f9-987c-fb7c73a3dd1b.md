[overview]
Fleshweaver is an Attonement Style Healer
This Class Uses Health, And Rage as it's main Resources.

After Extensive testing this is the best build i have come up with.

Made By Mortyy on Vol'jin

If you would like to join the Shrimp Gang community check out my friend Twitch.tv/Thighgapy

[talents]
The Reason We Skipped the Bottom Row Talents is as Follows
[Predator] Allows for your [Sanguine Rupture](coa-skill:bloodmage:800774) to do 10% more Critical Damage, and [Sanguine Mend](coa-skill:bloodmage:802310) to hit up to 2 targets instead of 1
Now if you Factor in you took the talent [Crimson Scion](coa-skill:bloodmage:806424) This is a very good combination allowing you to proc instant cast heals on up to 2 party members next to each other. 
By Dealing Damage with any Direct Damage abilities You can Proc [Crimson Scion](coa-skill:bloodmage:806424) for instant cast [Sanguine Mend](coa-skill:bloodmage:802310) a few examples of these abiltiies are [Sanguine Rupture](coa-skill:bloodmage:800774) , [Bloodmoon Blast](coa-skill:bloodmage:501607) , [Ravenous Strike](coa-skill:bloodmage:500123) , or [Claw Sweep](coa-skill:bloodmage:500169)
[Sanguine Mend](coa-skill:bloodmage:802310) is your main heal on the move.

We also Took [Adrenaline Boost] And [Flesh Foundry](coa-skill:bloodmage:704633) Because it Allows for us to increase our crit chance dramatically While Weaving in Cursed form abilities and allows us to fish for instant [Dark Liturgy](coa-skill:bloodmage:800781)  and allows us to keep up [Dark Essence] as long as [Dark Liturgy](coa-skill:bloodmage:800781) is active (Getting good at this will be extremely rewarding)

[rotation]
When Entering a Dungeon Apply These Buffs to your Party and to yourself
- Greater [Sanguinary Offering](coa-skill:bloodmage:706630)- Greater [Bloodthorns](coa-skill:bloodmage:802313)- [Blood Shield](coa-skill:bloodmage:504296)

Rotation
- [Sanguine Rupture](coa-skill:bloodmage:800774) To Generate Rage On Multiple Targets.
- [Crimson Tide](coa-skill:bloodmage:504282) is a good skill to use if you have Excess rage in a Aoe Pull Because it will apply bleeds on up to 10 enemies which can Proc  [Spice Of Life] This combined with [Raging Hunger] Allows for massive rage generation and Pooled vitality  in aoe scenarios it also adds [Waves of Blood] Which can also proc  [Excess Blood].
- [Ravenous Strike](coa-skill:bloodmage:500123) Quickest single target rage generator, Also Allows you to proc [Adrenaline Boost] to get a instant [Dark Liturgy](coa-skill:bloodmage:800781) Both [Ravenous Strike](coa-skill:bloodmage:500123) (single target) and [Claw Sweep](coa-skill:bloodmage:500169) (Aoe) Can trigger this. and since both of these are cursed form abilities you proc [Dark Essence] at a 100% up time
- [Claw Sweep](coa-skill:bloodmage:500169) This Ability Costs 22 rage it's a good ability to use in aoe pulls to trigger [Dark Essence] and [Adrenaline Boost], while in aoe pulls you can use this in conjunction with [Sanguine Rupture](coa-skill:bloodmage:800774) to fish for both [Adrenaline boost] and [Crimson Scion](coa-skill:bloodmage:806424)
-  [Adrenaline Boost] This is a Talent, When using any of your melee cursed form abilities, if you crit it allows you to cast [Dark Liturgy](coa-skill:bloodmage:800781) or [Bloodmoon Blast](coa-skill:bloodmage:501607) as an instant cast.
- [Dark Essence] This is a Talent, When you use any of your cursed form abilities it will trigger an aoe Hot on your party as long as [Dark Liturgy](coa-skill:bloodmage:800781) is active, if you actively melee weave you will have a 100% uptime on Hot's also triggering [Adrenaline Boost]  for the instant cast [Dark Liturgy](coa-skill:bloodmage:800781)
-  Casting [Dark Liturgy](coa-skill:bloodmage:800781) to apply [Blood Rituals](coa-skill:bloodmage:705729) is important so that you can use [Bloodmoon Blast](coa-skill:bloodmage:501607) for a party wide Heal while also generating rage (Single Target) While Stationary to avoid cleave mobs.
- Your Main Aoe heal is [Heartbreak](coa-skill:bloodmage:520314) This Costs 40 Rage.  If using this while Empowered it Gives 165 Attack Power/Spellpower To Party/Raid (Very good buff to keep up if you can fit it in.)
- Your main Single Target Heal is [Sanguine Mend](coa-skill:bloodmage:802310) This costs 20 Rage. If using this while Empowered it becomes a instant cast, and heals you for 50% of the heal value. Since we took [Predator] This ability now heals up to 2 people. and [Crimson Scion](coa-skill:bloodmage:806424) can proc when you do damage allowing you access to this ability as an instant cast.
- [Blood Veil] This is good to use Preemptively on a pull since it lasts 8 seconds or when your party is taking alot of damage, it shields them for 50% of incoming damage up to 1600 and gives you a bit of breathing room to generate rage or line up a [Heartbreak](coa-skill:bloodmage:520314) 
- [Fleshcraft](coa-skill:bloodmage:801952) This is your Panic button. It's Close to a lay on hands.
- [Shadow Vigil](coa-skill:bloodmage:806182) When someone in your party Rips threat or is taking alot of damage and they aren't a tank this will Give them an 8 second threat wipe.
- Before Using [Red Thirst](coa-skill:bloodmage:520493) Make sure you line up 10 stacks of pooled vitality and use [Heartbreak](coa-skill:bloodmage:520314) to Grant 165 attack power/Spellpower for 20 seconds. Combining this combo with [Infuse] Allows for Massive Damage for both you and your team.
- [Vampyr's Kiss](coa-skill:bloodmage:504275) applies a 2000 damage dot and instantly Gives you 30 Rage.
- [Blood Tap] Is a ability that Sacrafices some health for 50% of your maximum rage.
- [Animated Blood] When you have excess rage it is good to let these guys loose and put out some damage, they also heal you for 200 hp everytime each of them cast blood blast.
- [Lunge](coa-skill:bloodmage:500126) Good skill to close the gap on mobs so u can melee weave or if you need to catch up to tank 
- [Hypovolemic Shock](coa-skill:bloodmage:572305) This ability lowers the Attack power of an enemy by 265, and also removes a Enrage effect good to keep up on bosses if they are not immune.
