[overview]
This build is designed to hold Amazing Threat and phenomenal dps due to [Rotclaw](coa-skill:bloodmage:503476) now being able to stack. you can use [Monstrous Hunger](coa-skill:bloodmage:804811) as an offensive skill when paired with [Bloodfang Bite](coa-skill:bloodmage:800156) to generate around 20 instances of Call of the dark-wing this is also used as a fast and large self heal

[talents]
Rot claws stacking talent enables it to do insane dps and hold a lot of threat in the proccess

[rotation]
[Rotclaw](coa-skill:bloodmage:503476) and [Claw Sweep](coa-skill:bloodmage:500169) are used to move through instances and still hold aoe threat while on the move as  long as the healer can keep up.

When pulling absurdly large packs use [Apotheosis](coa-skill:bloodmage:804195) to remain immortal during the aoe as long as you deal any form of damage

If needed use [Fleshcraft](coa-skill:bloodmage:801952) as a quick HP filler 

if you think your threat is dropping in aoe use [Blood Howl](coa-skill:bloodmage:800782) and spam aoe 



BOSSES
Single target bosses are still easy to hold threat on as long as you use [Ravenous Bite (Packleader)](coa-skill:bloodmage:560365) /// [Rotclaw](coa-skill:bloodmage:503476) because it has a 3 stacking dot effect and [Bloodfang Bite](coa-skill:bloodmage:800156) paired with [Monstrous Hunger](coa-skill:bloodmage:804811) to generate Call of the darkwing
