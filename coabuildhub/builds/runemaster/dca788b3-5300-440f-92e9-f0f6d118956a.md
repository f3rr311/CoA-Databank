[overview]
Basic talent build for engravement PvE. Make sure to read the talent section for the Single Target version; you only need to switch a few talents. 

I am by no means an expert, just passing on what I do know because our class does not have any builds posted. I will update this build as needed. 

Weapon Engravings:
AoE - Earth + Fire
Single Target - Frost + Fire (or Fire + Fire; with the macro in the macro section, you can apply two without swapping weapons, similar DPS though)

[talents]
Class talents are fairly loose; feel free to swap around some talents if there is something you think you'll need for the content you're doing. 

**Switching to Single Target: **
Remove Sky and Stone, Fists of Power, and Stone Savant and pick up Echoes of Eternity and Runelord; the last talent point is up to you; Symbols of Power 1/2 is a small DPS increase, Guarding Rune is probably better typically, unless you know you won't need the extra defensive; it is a very strong one.

If you feel like you need Guarding Rune in AoE for your group, I'd drop Mark of Strength or Explosive Runes. I've seen people drop Magic Etchings, but unless your group has a good armor reduction, I don't think it's a great idea; it helps a lot with boss damage from the group, and bosses can take a little while to kill.

[rotation]
Rotation is pretty simple, will probably get a little more complicated as we get more gear and can afford mana tax with Tattoo swapping. The goal is to get as many procs with your weapon engravings as possible. 

We are very strong as funneling our damage into a main target and passively AoEing the pack off that target, make sure you have a high priority target selected when cleaving. We also have one of, if not the very best kick in the game, it has a short cast time, but don't be weird, kick. 

**Tattoos**
You'll basically sit in Water forever; if you have a little more gear and can keep up on mana regen, feel free to swap into Fire and back to Water as needed. Don't forget swapping to Air removes a slow.

**AoE: **
Pop Zenith as much as possible; when Zenith is up remove Fist of Ancients from the rotation. 
Runic Brand for Marks and pop them with Runeblade; press Primordial Blast to reset Runeblade charges and hope for Runic Brand reset procs so you can keep popping marks. Weave in Fist of the Ancients when you have no Runic Brand stacks and Primordial Blast is on CD; the damage is kind of low, but the 20% engraving change is good. 

The idea is to try and keep up as many Runic Brand marks as you can and pop them for explosions and Wild Steams, and then rely on earth/fire procs from weapons to fill in the rest.  

**Single Target**
The rotation is not massively different from AoE, but the priority list shuffles a bit. Pop Zenith as much as possible; if you're the single target build, you'll have 2 charges. 
Primordial Blast moves up in priority to the top; it hits pretty hard in single target and helps stack armor pen ASAP. From there, try to keep Runic Blade marks up as much as possible; you'll pop them with your Runeblade as filler and press Primordial Blast on cooldown. Weave in Fist of the Ancients when Runic Blade and Primordial Blast are on CD.
