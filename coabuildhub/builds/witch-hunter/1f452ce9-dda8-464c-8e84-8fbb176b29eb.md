[overview]
Solo build focused on surviving most encounters and being able to farm multiple mobs at once with ease

[talents]
Class spec: Combat Training 2/2 ->Night Stalker 2/2 -> Brimming Hatred -> Evil Never Sleeps -> Witchblood Tonic -> Follow the Edict -> Vengeful Intent -> Brand the unworthy -> Dark intuition ->Vampire Hunter. 
Rest is up to liking


BK spec: first focus on getting March of the Black King for proccing Shadow Brand with it, then unlock desecrate for multiple mob farm -> wide swings into Witchblight->Noctis Protection-> Dark Juggernaut

[rotation]
Early levels focus on attacking enemies with ranged then transition into melee combat
Use Noctis Blade whenever possible
Desecrate only when fighting more enemies
Burrow bolt to get caster into melee range

[stats]
Agility is most important then focus on spellpower, due to level 30 passive you should have enough Stamina to survive

[gear]
I recommend crafting your gear with leatherworking since its pretty decent

[enchants]
Agility Elixir and Defense since lower armor 
Scroll of Agility or Protection
