[overview]
This build specialises in AoE dungeon settings and is primarily using Twilight Frenzy for AoE and Witchbane for Single Target. If you've played Diablo III and enjoyed Demon Hunter, this could feel very familiar.
Build is highly effective provided the tank is pulling large packs of at least 3-4 mobs. Any less, and you won't have infinite rage to use Twilight Frenzy. 

**Pros:**
+High AoE capability
+Easy rotation
+High mobility
+AoE Stun/Silence

**Cons:**
-Spinning constantly
-Requires tank behaviour
-Poor survivability

[talents]
There are no Capstone talents for this build. The 25th point will go into Contract Killing. The majority of your casts will be Twilight Frenzy, and none of the Capstones play well with it during AoE sequences. You could pick up Bounty Hunter or Heavy Crossbows in its place for single target, but you'll get more out of Contract Killing overall in a dungeon setting.

**Tormentor + Strafing Shot:**
You can spread the Tormentor DoT to nearby enemies when you critically strike. This includes Twilight Frenzy. During AoE set up/the pull you can Darkslayer/Rage CD > Tormentor and then start Twilight Frenzy to spread. This will become even better when you hit level 50 (Tormenting the Tormented) and as you get more crit.

**Crossbow Frenzy:**
You will want the movement speed to keep up with certain tanks in a dungeon. Being able to catch up and use frenzy while the tank grabs more mobs to throw into the blender has very high potential damage.

**Everything you Have:**
More damage on Twilight Frenzy.

**Arbalest Mastery:**
On single target Witchbane is your main damage dealer. This will put a stacking debuff up to 10 on the target. Probably works with other WHs as well because it's a debuff on a target (ie Sunder). Get 50 rage and spam it. You can use Dark Regeneration/Tonics (with Regenerative Elixirs talent) to keep firing infinitely.

**Rapid:**
You don't need Witchbane to do AoE when you have Twilight Frenzy. Instead, make it Rapid so you can boost your boss and single target damage.

**Bane of Witches:**
Both Witchbane and Twilight Frenzy are physical damage. Heartseeking Arrow is primarily used on CD during Single Target for the +10% physical damage dealt. You may also get some use out of it as an effective filler cast while the tank is rounding up mobs and it isn't safe to Twilight Frenzy yet.

[rotation]
## AoE Rotation
While the tank grabs the mobs use Darkslayer once for rage or Heartseeking Bolt if you have more than 50 rage. Use Tormentor and then get ready to blend. Use Scourge/Shadow Trap once everything is stacked tight. Use Scourge Trap/Holy Water Tonic for undead/demons.

- Darkslayer/Heartseeking Bolt > Tormentor > Dark Regeneration/Holy Water Tonic > Twilight Frenzy until everything stacked > Scourge/Shadow Trap > Twilight Frenzy (until only 1 mob left)


## Single Target Rotation
You are keeping the Heartseeking Bolt +10% phys damage buff up on yourself, ensuring Tormentor is always ticking and using Witchbane as much as possible. Use your rage generators (tonics/Dark Regen) to avoid having to Darkslayer as required.

- Heartseeking Bolt > Tormentor > Dark Regeneration/Holy Water Tonic > Witchbane x6 > Refresh Tormentor + Caltrops > Refresh Heartseeking Bolt > Witchbane x6 > Refresh Tormentor

[tips]
Feeling overwhelmed by the amount of abilities? You can basically take Shadowblast off your bars.

You can cancel your Twilight Frenzy channel by using an instant cast.

Use your traps! The Scourge Trap is actually insane damage on Undead mobs and Brand of the Damned is basically a better Shadow Word: Death.

Some tanks get shitty when you start attacking before they've hit the mob. Just be aware and try to space yourself. If it's unsafe, don't Twilight Frenzy yet. Build some rage, get a Heartseeking buff. Ideally, the tank should be hitting the mobs as they run by so you can Twilight Frenzy almost immediately even as they continue to gather mobs up.
