Basic set up is fairly straight forward. Brand of the unworthy to get to the target, into shadow trap. During the trap, Pyro Tonic, Flourish, Fiery Judgment, Torchlight+Darkslayers lantern,  dawn blade, pe, spam dusk blade. Dark regen off cd where you know you are not killing soon. Idea here is to stun into stun since they are both off gcd, just make sure there is no gap and to silence out. 

Also, your a rage class. Keep up flames of sin between goes. uptime is king with brushin' off the dusk and dusk blade has 100% pen and heals so it's imperative you are hitting something. Also generate your stacks and pop the enemy like a zit with torch. 

Use bomb wisely. CC the healer or off dps and towards the end of the cc you bomb. Especially op if the healer still has trinket and the kill target doesn't.

Agi + spell power is better for fire spells, and int + attack power is better for stake. Torchlight and fiery judgment benefit from both. therefore we prio hybrid items with more overall stats but agi is king. So I think seargent's linked is good for honor. just my opinion we'll know more later. Also allows for ranged damage if you need to kite a go.
