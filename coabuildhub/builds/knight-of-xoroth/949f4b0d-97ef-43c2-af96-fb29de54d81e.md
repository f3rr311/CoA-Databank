[overview]
Can change 1 rank of invasion for either + one of Infernal Pursuit or + one Shadowflame Forge.

[rotation]
Melt with 6 Demonfire -> Skulltaker with 6 Demonfire (to increase Hellmaw tickrate) -> Demon Heart -> Hellmaw with 6 Demonfire -> pop burst (Hellfire Form + Blood Fury if orc + Burning rage) -> then spam Seeking flame and alternate with annihilation.

I advise you to create a macro for annihilation because it's a little bugged.

I use this ones:

#showtooltip Infernal Strike
/cast Annihilation
/cast Infernal Strike

#showtooltip Seeking flame
/cast Annihilation
/cast Seeking flame
