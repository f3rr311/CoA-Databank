[overview]
Great balanced build both for AOE and ST in dungeons and M+

[talents]
Hellstorm is not taken in this build due to it being slightly worse than your usual AOE Demonfire spender Flames of Xoroth and on top of that, there is a great talent to have instead of it which is [Punisher](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:704988) that helps with ST damage, especially while usuing your [Hellfire Form](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:503300). One of the most powerful AOE in this build is Into the Maw that is used with both [Indiscriminate](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:704958) and [Shadowflame Forge](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:704989) 1/2 in this build which is enough to use [Seeking Flame](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:805671) 3 times after applying your Hellmaw on your target. This way your Hellmaw spreads on 10 targets total right before exploding with huge damage

[rotation]
Always use your [Pestilence of Apocalypse](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:804786), don't forget to buff Str Mark and call your [Call: Hellfire Imp](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:804883). The Annihilation proc always takes priority to generate Demonfire stacks.
AOE with [Hellfire Form](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:503300):
Start with using your [Burning Rage](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:520295) before the pull. Firstly use your [Hellfire Form](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:503300) and right after that [Unleash Pestilence](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:801002) (try to always refresh it's duration by spending 4+ Demonfire stacks on Flames of Xoroth or Hellmaw) Then use one [Meatsaw](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:501508) to get 6 stacks of Demonfire and right after that apply your Hellmaw on the tankiest target and use enough [Seeking Flame](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:805671) on your Hellfire target to spread Hellfire on every target that was pulled. If your [Unleash Pestilence](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:801002) is at 1-2 seconds until expiring then use your Flames of Xoroth to refresh it's duration and then continue spreading Hellmaw if needed. After spreading your Hellmaw on every target use Flames of Xoroth and then continue spamming [Seeking Flame](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:805671) to stack Demonfire if your [Hellfire Form](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:503300) is still active and spend your Demonfire stacks on Flames of Xoroth if your Hellfire is still up.
AOE when you don't have your [Hellfire Form](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:503300):
Use your [Meatsaw](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:501508) to gain Demonfire stacks and use them on Hellmaw spreading it 3 times by using your [Seeking Flame](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:805671) 3 times on CD. In between of using [Seeking Flame](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:805671) continue using your [Meatsaw](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:501508) to gain Demonfire stacks and Flames of Xoroth to spend your Demonfire stacks if your Hellmaw is still up, othewise use Hellmaw and spread it once again since it still blows up if the target dies dealing more damage than Flames of Xoroth.
ST with [Hellfire Form](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:503300):
Start with using your [Burning Rage](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:520295) before the pull. Firstly use your [Hellfire Form](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:503300) and right after that [Unleash Pestilence](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:801002) (try to always refresh it's duration by spending 4+ Demonfire stacks on Melt, Hellmaw and Skullsplitter) Then spam your [Seeking Flame](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:805671) with 6 stacks of Demonfire until your [Hellfire Form](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:503300) ends. After your [Hellfire Form](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:503300) ends, firstly spend your Demonfire stacks on Melt, then use [Demon Heart](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:805669) and apply Hellmaw. Use your Infernal Strike to get Demonfire stacks and cast your [Seeking Flame](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:805671) at 6 Demonfire stacks when it's not on cooldown before spending your stacks on Skulltaker since [Seeking Flame](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:805671) deals great damage at 6 Demofire stacks thanks to Brimstone Flames. Try to keep Melt on the target all the time and reapply Hellmaw after the explosion.
ST when you don't have your [Hellfire Form](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:503300):
Use your Infernal Strike to gain Demonfire stacks and use them on Melt, Hellfire and Skulltaker. Cast your [Seeking Flame](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:805671) at 6 Demonfire stacks when it's not on cooldown before spending your stacks on Skulltaker since [Seeking Flame](coa-skill:knight-of-xoroth:805671) deals great damage at 6 Demofire stacks thanks to Brimstone Flames. Try to keep Melt on the target all the time and reapply Hellmaw after the explosion.

[stats]
I am still not 100% sure about the prio, but imo it's something like this:
Strenght > AP > SP > Int > Stamina (try to get every single item with Strenght, but items with AP and no main stat are also great)
Items with Agility and Spirit are absolutely irrelevant.
As for the secondary stats you should consider getting enough Hit to play comfortably (at least get enough spell hit for now which is not that hard to get with Mythic gear):
Hit > Crit > ARP = Haste
Not sure if ARP or Haste is better for Hellfire, Either way it's better to get Hit or Crit

[gear]
