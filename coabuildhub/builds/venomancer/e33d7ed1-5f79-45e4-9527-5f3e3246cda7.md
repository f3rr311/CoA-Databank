[overview]
I'll add more info when I get more time.

This is my current Venomancer Tank build.

You can only use two vemon's at once. As a tank you'll use Weakening Venom and Blight Venom.
- Weakening Venom ( 35% chance to deal 80 shadow damage and reduce the enemy's attack power by 241 for 12 sec)
- Blight Venom ( 30% chance to poison an enemy dealing 1852 shadow damage over 9 sec)

You have a choice to use Greater Spider Pheromones (+92 Agil) or Greater Beetle Pheromones ( + Armor 343 and all primary stats by 14). For mythics, I just use spider pheromones as you get more attack power, 3crit hit, increased armor, and increased Dodge chance.


#1- In the Class tree- when picking talents do not pick Shadra's Lair. As a tank when you use Burrow or the Lair you lose agro and the mobs go straight to the highest threat which is normally the healer.  The only reason to use this is to hide from a big one-shot boss ability. I picked up burrow only because I didn't see another useful talent to pick.

#2- In the class tree - I picked Chitinous Spikes over the only other viable pick which is Venomizer.  With Venomizer I don't have any issues with my current proc chance, so I'm thinking the extra reflective damage is best for threat and dps.

#3- In the Fortitude Tree pick Impale, this is not only useful but I find it fun to position the mobs in a line and then impale them. It does a ton of damage and stuns the mobs, bonus it looks cool.

#4- In the Fortitdue tree- although the Barbed stinger looks good, I don't find it useful enough to waste a talent point on it. I do have 1 point in wicked barbs because it applies Wicked poison when you use Chitin Rush which i use when it is up.

[rotation]
Pull: Use Hive Instinct if you have no rage.
Chitin Rush, Carapace Crash, Claw Strike, Venomtip Poison, Carapace Regeneration

When your rage gets low, use Claw Strike. As long as you never run out of range, your Carapace Regeneration will stay active. Best practice is to never get to 3 stacks as it drains rage 10 rage every second per stack. 1 stack only 10 rage per second, which is easy to maintain. 3 stacks is 30 rage per second, and you'll be empty before you know it. For big AOE groups, you can stack to 2, and you should be good.

Line up the mobs and use Impale.

Use Chitin rush to do more damage and threat. Be careful not to agro another group or use it to get more mobs to tank,

Use vile sting whenever it is up. This is a free Venom trigger.

If you have no health concerns, use expulsion for extra DPS. If your healer is having issues keeping you topped off, then save your stacks of exposed flesh for your defensive cooldowns.

If you run out of rage, hit Hive Instinct for 100 rage (2 min cooldown)

If your health starts to drop use use your Defensive Cooldown
- if you have high exposed flesh stack use Regrow Exoskeleton - reduce damage by 20% and an additional 1 sec per stack.
- if below 50% use Harden- reduce damage by 45%, decaying quickly over 10 sec and healing for 1% maximum health each time a stack is removed(3 minute cooldown)
- Use lifeblood- reduce damage 20%. 
Try to alternate your defensive cooldowns; try not to blow them all at once.

For spell interruption, you have two options:
1- Nullifying Toxin (16 sec cooldown)
2- Impale (40 sec cooldown )

[stats]
Stamina>Agility>Attack power>Spell Power>Intel

Stamina gives you Health and increases the damage of Claw Strike. The key to this build is stacking Stamina. When you use Carapace Regeneration, that means you restore 1% of maximum health every 1 sec. The more health, the more regeneration. For example, I'm at around 12K health right now. When I have 2 stacks of Carapace regeneration up then I'm healing myself 240 health per sec. 

Agility gives you attack power, Critical hit Chance, increases Armor, and Increases Dodge

Intelligence increases Spell Damage. Intellect directly scales your Nature damage and deadly poisons, allowing your Damage-over-Time (DoT) effects and venom strikes to hit significantly harder.

Spell power increases Damage-over-Time (DoT) Spells: Scales the damage of your toxic poisons

Strength does give you attack power, but not as much as Agility.
