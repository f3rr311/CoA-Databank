[overview]
Witch doctor: Brewing is not an easy healer. It has a lot of instants and HOTs, you require A LOT of spirit to feel your heal and your mana really well. You'll struggle leveling but at the end its worth it

the main ingredients are usually jungle shrooms and frog bones for the HOT and absorbs on allies while also providing with healing over time near you,

[talents]
i prefer this set of talents since it provides a hell of a lot of heals. The cauldron gives a totem like heal that basically makes them unkillable

but you could go for the bases instead if you feel like  you want to spam spirit in a bottle (atm of writting this tiki splash feels bugged)

[rotation]
just make sure to not spam your abilities such a throw potion or splash potion and use your mojos (Mojo: Jungle Thistle and Mojo: Frog Shrooms) as an emergency in case people are dying.

cauldron is good to be used in constant damage zones or as an oopsie button

mojo beam to aoe heal and spirit in a bottle to get spirit and heal

also your loa's brew is good for a few buffs

[stats]
Spirit (ofc) > Intellect > Spell power >Haste > etc

[leveling]
at the beginning you can take Mojo Addiction if you feel like you dont have enough mana, but once at a comfortable level just take it out since theres better uses for those 2 talent points
