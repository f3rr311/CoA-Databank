Overview

This build focuses more on AoE damage using 5 Raise: Ghoul and March of the Dead instead of big single target boss damage like my other guide. This build works VERY well with a tank that likes to pull big and preferably LoS the mobs around a corner or against a wall. Also is great for leveling in dungeons if you have people who can do single target damage.

⚔️

Rotation

Opener

  • Glacial Ward
  • Greater Grim Mandate (Greater Foul Mandate if you already have Grim Mandate from someone else)
  • Greater Razorice & Greater Chill of the Tomb (Optional)
  • 5 Raise: Ghoul
  • Cap Runic Power with Glacial Tap or Runic Harvest

Once tank plants

  • Call of the Grave
  • Animate: Skeletal Archer (Big 25% haste buff)
  • Animate: Bone Wraith
  • Animate: Plaguefather
  • Animate: Frost Wyrm
  • Unholy Frenzy
  • March of the Dead (3x)
  • Glacial Tap
  • Runic Harvest
  • March of the Dead (3x)

(Rinse and Repeat)

You may need to use Crypt Swarm for building Runic Power.

This rotation works very well if tank pulls massive and goes behind a wall, plant yourself so when the March of the Dead spawns, they will take like 1 or 2 steps and either collide with a wall or the pack of mobs (the tighter the pack, the better).

Another option is not using March of the Dead and focusing on your Command: Undead for your ghouls to do your AoE. Practically giving you infinite mana if the pull is big enough. Runic Harvest works a lot better with these ghouls since there is more to drain from.

📊

Stats

Priority

  1. Hit Rating (Get to 16% or 128/128)
  2. Spell Power = Spell Damage
  3. Critical Strike Rating (10-12%~)
  4. Haste Rating (10-12%~)
  5. Intellegence
  6. Stamina

Leveling

Early leveling, I recommend 1 Raise: Abomination with rest being Skeletal Warrior. Once you unlock Raise: Ghoul you will swap all your summons to just Ghouls. They give you insane self healing since each auto they do heals you and each Command: Undead you get even more healing, turning excess healing into an absorb shield making you feel unkillable thanks to Life For Power.
