Overview

This is a guide for MDI style leveling ( Giga pulling and doing high dmg (usually top in the dung unless playing with a primalist/cultist/templar)

This class in this context is focused around applying debuffs and kiting. Plays very similar to VDH S1 in SL.

🌟

Talents

Class tree priorities:

  1. Lifeblood
  2. Versatile and Deadly
  3. Fury of Shadra & Avatar of shadra

Spec Tree priorities:

  1. Regrow Exoskeleton.
  2. Venomtip Poison
  3. Impale.
  4. Slimy stingers
  5. Venomics

U can play Shadra's gift for 5% st if u want.

Wicked Poison / Beetle poison is around 4 and 2% aoe respectively , 2%/1%st.

Drop Beetle poison for Catalyst or Alacrity if you want.

U can drop points from the 2 points nodes to get beetle poison/ deadly sting if u want.

U will be kiting most of the time even in higher keys it seems so how much u need defensive stats is still questionable.

U really want to be able live the grouping part of the pull after that you should be fine with decent play.

NOTE: EXPECT TO DIE A LOT WHILE LEARNING THIS. ESPECIALLY IF U WANT TO PULL LIKE A MADMAN (Which u definately can)

UPDATED TALENTS AFTER TESTING.

Alternative: Drop Venomancy Expert, Fury of anub and Mamba mentality for Blightcap Injestion.

⚔️

Rotation

USE: Spider Pheromones, Slow Poison + Blight Poison, and Venom Shield.

For The biggest pulls ( u can do most dungs in 2-3 pulls)

  1. Get impale as soon as possible
  2. Run Slow + Blight poison (if u are going to be face tanking run Weakening Venom , for pure st Adrenal Venom)
  3. Pull big, impale all the mobs to let ur healer catch up with the pull dmg. U can use Defenisves as needed

what u do is: 1. Stun the mobs apply all ur dmg and slows. 2. Use defensive after stun ends. 3. after u see bad debuffs getting stacked on u or u feel like ur losing hp. Chitin rush away and kite. Then go back in use stuns on cd, and defensives as needed depening on situation.

Perfectly u can tank packs for only 2-3 globals at a time especially with Venomics later u can basiacally play like SL S1 VDH. And u will be top dps as well in most leveling scenarios.

AOE DPS Priority:

  1. Expulsion
  2. Cerapace Crash 1x for slimy stingers
  3. Locust Swarm once the pack is grouped.
  4. Barbed stinger on prio target (recast at 15 Stacks)
  5. Keep Venomtip poison up (usually you pull with this to soften the dmg you take when pulling)
  6. Spam Cerapace Crash
  7. Claw strike if no rage (shouldnt happen too often)

ST DPS Priority:

  1. Barbed Stinger on CD(recast at 15stacks)
  2. Deadly Stinger Hivebreak Proc
  3. 5stack+ Expulsion
  4. Keep Venomtip up
  5. Spam hivebreak
  6. Clawstrike if no rage.

Stats

No idea yet. For leveling prioritise stamina, agi, and armor.

Agi is ur best overall stat as it provides armor, dodge, AP and SP, and crit.

Best dps stat is hit rating till cap while leveling, spell power after that.

Your base spell values are high enough to top dps every aoe pull.
