Build Guide

📋

Overview

Thoughts on the Templar Crusader: It's tanky and can deal insane damage at the same time because of crit. Not to mention, it can sustain itself with little to no healing from a dedicated healer. However, it's a combo oriented class, and a misclick can mean the very end of you. Also, gears are hard to come by since mail armor is a traditionally STR-STA stat gear. However, once you optimize your gear, you'll have a fun time watching mobs fall to you.

For combo build up (your oaths):

This build only relies on combo skills, Righteous Lunge and Cleave. Lunge gives you a 10% additional damage and healing for each stack. Cleave on the other hand allows you to hit an additional enemy for each stack. Now it is important to remember that Cleave stacks only affect combo finishers and other damage-dealing skills that do not rely on oaths (cleave into lunge doesn't give you 2 lunge stacks).

Main Damage Skills:

Our main damage comes from Righteous Tempest, Argent Blade, Scarlet Hammer and Titanstrike.

Righteous Tempest - aoe damage (but also useful for single dps), draw mobs in with threat and mini black hole (if you know Dota 2) from Templar Rituals spec

Argent Blade - crit damge and health sustain

Scarlet Hammer - damage and energy sustain

Titanstrike - finisher damage due to high crit chance (Judgment of the Gods spec) and health sustain

It is important to note that Tempest is our only combo finisher skill. This is why it is important to reset your oaths every time Tempest is used. It is equally worth noting that Tempest (as well as other combo finisher skill), relies on the actual number of oaths active, not the number of different kinds of oaths active (3 stacks of Cleave deals the same Tempest damage as a Cleave, a Lunge and a Condemn active). That is why it only important to only use Lunge and Cleave to maximize damage while maintaining energy efficiency.

For rotations, see Rotations tab

Rotation

Rotations would only depend if it's single target DPS or AOE DPS

For single target:

Opener:

Lunge -> Lunge -> Tempest

If Argent Blade procs:

Lunge -> Lunge -> Argent Blade -> Scarlet Hammer -> repeat AB-SH if it is still proc-ing, otherwise go Tempest

If Argent Blade does not proc:

Lunge -> Lunge -> Argent Blade -> Scarlet Hammer (if SH procs)

Lunge -> Lunge -> Argent Blade -> Tempest

This is not a strict formula because we want to use AB-SH combo as much as possible when AB procs. In fact, use AB-BH everytime it procs as long as you have a Lunge or 2 active.

Finisher (Titanstrike available):

Lunge -> Lunge -> Titanstrike (skip lunges if there are oaths still active) -> AB-SH (if it procs and repeat if AB procs after SH use until AB no longer procs), otherwise use Tempest -> Lunge -> Titanstrike (off cooldown)

For multiple targets:

Opener:

Lunge -> Lunge -> Tempest

If Argent Blade procs:

Lunge -> Cleave (1-2 times depending if two or more than 3 mobs) -> Argent Blade -> Scarlet Hammer -> repeat AB-SH combo as long as AB still procs -> Tempest

If Argent Blade does not proc:

Lunge -> Cleave (1-2 times depending if two or more than 3 mobs) -> Argent Blade -> Scarlet Hammer (if it procs) -> Tempest

Lunge -> Cleave (1-2 times depending if two or more than 3 mobs) -> Argent Blade -> Tempest

Finisher (Titanstrike available):

Lunge -> Cleave (1-2 times depending if two or more than 3 mobs) -> Titanstrike (skip oaths if oaths still active) -> AB-SH (if it procs), otherwise use Tempest -> Titanstrike (off cooldown)

Like with single-target DPS, we want to use and abuse AB-SH combo and keep hitting AB-SH to multiple enemies until AB no longer procs and then go Tempest.

Why only 2-3 oaths?

In theory, I'm not thinking about damage dealing (you have a high crit rate, the damage comes naturally) but enemy damage mitigation, energy management and health sustain with skills and their respective specs. Enemies will eventually fall but we have to stay alive and be able to have the energy to use our skills, especially if you're just solo-ing.

Why only 1-2 Cleave stacks?

Same reason as above. You'll have to also prioritize staying alive for solo runs.

How about reaching your target?

Unfortunately, we only have Divine Force and Silverhand Incantation as gap-closers. Use them if available before opening with Lunge.
