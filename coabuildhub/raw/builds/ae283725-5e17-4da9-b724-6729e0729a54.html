Overview

Ever wanted to feel like a raid boss?

That's exactly what this build is about.

You're trading flashy burst damage for ridiculous survivability, allowing you to tank almost anything the game throws at you while still putting out enough DPS to stay relevant.

The build shines in every PvE scenario and can easily be adapted for PvP by replacing a couple of optional picks with Mana Burn.

Everything else is essentially mandatory. Every talent has a purpose and contributes to making the build as durable and consistent as possible.

If dying isn't part of your game plan, you've found the right build.

🌟

Talents

This build focuses on consistency rather than burst damage.

Every talent has been selected with one of four objectives:

  • Increase survivability.
  • Improve Felfury generation.
  • Maximize Inner Demon uptime.
  • Maintain strong threat while dealing competitive damage.

Many offensive talents were intentionally skipped in favor of defensive consistency. A dead tank deals no damage.

The Tyrant tree naturally provides excellent damage, allowing us to invest more heavily into mitigation without sacrificing clear speed.

If you prefer a simpler rotation with one less button to manage, you can replace Tyrant's Gaze with Betrayer. While you'll lose some execute damage, Betrayer remains a solid alternative and is perfectly viable for players who value a smoother playstyle.

⚔️

Rotation

Multi-Target Rotation

Hateforged Barrier → Burning Hatred or Blood of Mannoroth → Inner Demon → Felrend → Felrend → Sunder → Sargeron Smite

Use Carve whenever possible. Thanks to Annuhilan Rituals, a fully channeled Carve effectively costs only 1 Felfury, making it extremely efficient. Follow it with Fireball to benefit from the empowered cast. The Carve → Fireball combo deals excellent AoE damage and should be used whenever you have the opportunity.

⚠️⚠️ Warning: The Carve → Fireball combo is a DPS gain, but it also comes with a survivability trade-off.⚠️⚠️

Because you're spending less Felfury with the comboFelrend and Sargeron Smite, you reduce your Hateforged Barrier uptime, gain fewer Abyssal Conditioning stacks, and spend more time channeling. If you're playing without a dedicated healer, I generally recommend sticking to the Felrend + Sunder + Sargeron rotation for much better consistency and survivability. See the discussion in the comments for a more detailed explanation.

Once an enemy drops below 35% Health, prioritize Tyrant's Gaze to execute the target and quickly move on to the next enemy.

Single-Target Rotation

The single-target rotation follows the exact same priority, with only two changes:

  • Replace Felrend with Twin Slice.
  • Replace Sunder with Sargeron Smite.

Rotation:

Hateforged Barrier → Burning Hatred → Inner Demon → Twin Slice → Twin Slice → Sargeron Smite

⚠️ Never cast Sargeron Smite without Inner Demon active.

Doing so results in a massive DPS loss and significantly reduces the effectiveness of the build. Always make sure Inner Demon is active before using Sargeron Smite.

💥 Cooldowns

Hateforged Barrier

Your most important defensive cooldown.

Keep it active as often as possible. Thanks to cooldown reduction, maintaining nearly permanent uptime is entirely achievable.

Inner Demon

This build revolves around Inner Demon.

Never let it expire unless absolutely unavoidable.

Every aspect of your rotation is designed around maximizing its uptime.

Burning Hatred

Primarily used to instantly generate Felfury and quickly reactivate Inner Demon.

Avoid using it immediately on every pull.

Instead, save it for:

  • Long encounters.
  • Large pulls.
  • Recovering after losing Felfury.

Blur

Reserved for boss mechanics or emergency situations.

Since Blur reduces your threat generation, always follow it with Betray to immediately recover aggro.

Fel Bargain

Emergency button.

Only use it if your healer cannot keep you alive.

Without a Tinker in your group, this cooldown becomes significantly more dangerous.

Stats

Priority

  1. Hit Rating (until cap)
  2. Agility
  3. Stamina
  4. Critical Strike
  5. Haste

Agility is by far the strongest primary stat for this build, increasing both your offensive and defensive capabilities.

Because several abilities scale from weapon damage, using two slow two-handed weapons provides the highest overall performance.

Leather gear with Agility remains the best option.

📈

Leveling

Leveling as a Tyrant is extremely smooth and beginner-friendly. The spec has excellent survivability, allowing you to comfortably solo most quests and elite enemies without much difficulty.

Your main priority while leveling is to spam Felrend as your primary damage and threat ability while keeping Hateforged Barrier active whenever possible.

Although the build is capable of handling large pulls, don't get too greedy early on. Your gear won't be optimized yet, and overpulling can quickly overwhelm both you and your healer in dungeons.

Play it safe during the early levels, focus on maintaining your defensive abilities, and gradually increase your pull size as your gear and survivability improve.

By the time you reach higher levels and unlock the full build, you'll be able to comfortably chain large packs and fully take advantage of Tyrant's incredible durability.

🏴

PvP

The PvP version keeps almost the entire build unchanged.

The only recommended adjustment is replacing a few PvE-oriented talents with Mana Burn.

This allows you to heavily pressure healers and casters while maintaining the incredible survivability that makes Tyrant so powerful.

>_>

Tyrant truly shines as an objective-focused flag carrier. Your incredible survivability makes you extremely difficult to kill, allowing you to secure and transport flags with confidence.

While this build is perfectly capable of winning most 1v1 encounters, don't get baited into unnecessary fights. Your real value comes from playing the objective.

Capture bases, carry flags, waste the enemy team's time, and force them to commit multiple players just to bring you down. Winning the battleground is far more satisfying than chasing kills.

Tips

# ❌ Common Mistakes

  • Letting Inner Demon expire.
  • Casting Sargeron Smite without Inner Demon.
  • Forgetting to refresh Hateforged Barrier.
  • Wasting Burning Hatred at the beginning of every fight.
  • Using Fel Bargain too early.
  • Using Blur without immediately following it with Betray.
  • Ignoring Tyrant's Gaze below 35% Health.
  • Focusing too much on DPS instead of maintaining defensive uptime.

# 💡 Advanced Tips

  • Always start difficult pulls with Hateforged Barrier already active.
  • Plan your Burning Hatred usage instead of treating it like a regular cooldown.
  • Prioritize maintaining Inner Demon over squeezing in one extra offensive ability.
  • Use Felrend and Sunder aggressively on large pulls to stabilize threat before your DPS unloads.
  • Save Blur for predictable boss mechanics rather than random damage.
  • A good Tyrant anticipates incoming damage instead of reacting once health starts dropping.
  • Tyrant's Gaze is more than an execute—it helps reduce incoming damage by removing dangerous enemies from the fight faster.
  • Don't be afraid to chain large pulls. This build truly shines when fighting multiple enemies at once, where its survivability and threat generation outperform most other tanks.

hesediel·8 days ago
One thing worth mentioning is that while the Carve → Fireball combo does increase your DPS, it comes at the cost of survivability. Because you're spending less Felfury on your regular spenders, you generate fewer Hateforged Barrier cooldown reductions, which means lower barrier uptime and you're much more likely to take heavy damage during large pulls. It also adds two extra buttons to the rotation, whereas the Felrend + Sargeron Smite core already provides excellent damage while spending Felfury efficiently and restoring Energy. Don't forget Abyssal Conditioning either. Every time you cast a Felfury spender, you gain 5% increased Energy regeneration for 10 seconds, stacking up to 4 times. By continuously cycling Felrend and Sargeron Smite, you maintain those stacks much more consistently, which makes the rotation smoother and further improves both survivability and sustained damage.

hesediel·10 days ago
Hey! Thanks! 😊 Yes, the build is fully up to date. As for leveling, there isn't really a strict path to follow. The main goal is simply to reach Tyrant's Gaze as early as possible. For the Tyrant tree, I'd recommend taking ASAP : - Pure Hatred - Felrend - Infernal Constitution - Burning Hatred - Bulwark of Azzinoth For the Felsworn class tree, just follow the level 60 build and work your way down from Hateforged Barrier to Fel Communion, prioritizing the entire right side of the tree. In general, if a talent gives you damage reduction, survivability, or helps maintain your defenses, it's probably the right choice while leveling. For gear, don't overthink it. Just equip the best upgrades you find. Early on, I like stacking a bit more Stamina for extra comfort, then gradually switch toward Agility once better gear starts dropping. Finally, spam dungeons whenever you can, and everything should go just fine. 😄
