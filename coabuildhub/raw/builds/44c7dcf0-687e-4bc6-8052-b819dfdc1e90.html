Overview

Currently, the Animation tree offers the only truly viable spec to compete at a high level in PvP on Conquest of Azeroth. Because of the inherent risk of Necromancer becoming completely overpowered if over-tuned, the class has historically seen heavy tuning. However, with the right setup, Animation offers top-tier minion pressure, control, and explosive burst.

Abomination and Winds of Northrend are currently dead talents in this build.

This build uses 6 Ghouls.

Currently testing a promising BG version that is outperforming this version in BG.

Talents

Talent selection varies slightly based on your personal preference, the current meta, and whether you are queuing 1v1 duels or multi-target Battlegrounds.

Death’s Due: Less valuable in BGs, but theoretically useful in 1v1s to purge invulnerabilities. However, extensive testing shows it currently fails to reliably purge effects like the Venomancer’s invulnerability. It is recommended to drop this talent until further bug fixes land.

Gravebound Champion CD Reduction: Skip this for 1v1s, as rounds end far too fast to get a second cast. It holds much more value in drawn-out BGs.

Overwhelming Force: The superior option for baseline Mana and Runic Power generation.

Phylactery: Promising on paper, but currently untested in top-tier play and rarely utilized effectively. May be very strong in 2v2 and 3v3.

Frost Wyrm Pathing: Frost Wyrm burst is simply too powerful to pass up. To reach it, you are forced to path through a dead talent: Winds of Northrend (Lichfrost Slow).

Fetid Ward: A solid defensive option, though further testing is required to confirm if it outperforms alternative defensive talents.

Depravity vs. Mindless Fury: Depravity is currently the stronger pick, though Mindless Fury can be considered if you are heavily stacking Haste.

Tomb King vs. Bone Wrath: Tomb King provides significantly higher impact during your primary burst combo.

Unrelenting: Turns your Crypt Swarm into a machine gun for Runic Power generation.

Rotation

Buffs & Pre-Fight Setup

Minions = 6 Ghouls.

Baseline Buff Sequence

Keep these active at all times. Use a /castsequence macro to cycle through them quickly before 1v1 rounds or upon spawning in BGs:

Fetid Ward

Greater Grim Mandate

Greater Razorice

Greater Chill of the Tomb

Combat Stance & Auras

Assault Stance: Keep this active continuously for the +5% Critical Strike Chance and increased movement speed for your Ghouls.

Frozen Reliquary: Save this for when your Mana pool is completely depleted to maximize the missing-mana scaling benefit.

Core Opener & Rotation

Pre-Fight Prep: Drop your Summoning Ritual and channel Siphon Mana on your Ghouls to secure an early resources

Summon Archers: Always open by summoning your Skeleton Archers. They boost your Haste, deal damage from range, and build early Mana and Runic Power.

Apply Harvest Plague: Cast Harvest Plague immediately to begin siphoning health. The DoT tick actively prevents stealth classes from resetting the fight.

Summon Heavy Minions: Call forth your Tomb King and Plaguefather.

Execute the Burst Combo: Once your opponent commits to the fight and enters melee range of your army or you think you can kill them, pop your macro burst (Unholy Frenzy + Gravebound Champion + Frost Wyrm + Any racial trait) while commanding all ghouls to attack.

📊

Stats

Gear & Stat Hierarchy

Stamina: Priority #1 for PvP survivability to prevent getting burst down in stun locks.

Intellect: Primary stat for raw Spell Power, minion scaling, and Mana pool size.

Spell Hit Rating: Mandatory up to the PvP cap (4% / ~32–36 rating at level 60) so crucial CC and burst spells do not miss.

Haste / Critical Strike: Secondary stats to stack based on personal preference (Crit for burst potential, Haste for faster cast cycles).

I've moved away from Crit and have focused on Haste, we can get down to 0.5 sec on a Ghoul summon. Ghouls also go berserk with the extra Haste. Feels much better than crit.

🛡️

Gear / BiS

Horde/Alliance Insignia for CC cleanse (you can buy this tricket with Honor at the PvP vendor). This is Goated.

Main thing is to stop the perma CC from some classes you will face in 1v1.

Always buy PvP gear with honor and start stacking PvP Power to stand a chance.

Once you are level 60, you can spam BGs and the Call Board Daily for Honor and that will help you get the base PvP needed.

Macros

Macro I use since it has 3 instant casts that don't trigger global CD and one instant cast that does at the end.

(Unholy Frenzy + Gravebound Champion + Frost Wyrm + Any instant cast racial trait).

Copy and paste the macro below but you will need to remove or replace Arcane Torrent if you are not a Blood Elf:

#showtooltip Animate: Frost Wyrm

/cast Arcane Torrent

/cast Animate: Frost Wyrm

/cast Gravebound Champion

/cast Unholy Frenzy

Tips

Utility & Advanced Tactics

Defensive Tithe Window: Pop Bone Tithe at around 50% HP. This allows you to stack the shield while maximizing incoming heals from Fetid Ward and Command Attack from Ghouls.

Gravebound Defensive Offense: Weave Gravebound Champion into your burst phase to reduce incoming damage while adding another body to your minion army.

Whenever your attack commands give you a free cast proc:

Cast Blight if the target still has a large health pool.

Cast Frost Lich to snare fleeing enemies and/or generate a fast burst of Runic Power.

Runic Power Dumping: When your Runic Power caps out, spam your Command abilities.

Minion Recovery: If your army gets wiped, immediately cast Call of the Grave and resume spamming Ghouls.

Disruption Control:

Ghoulify: Exceptional for catching targets in BGs and interrupting spell casts while your army attacks.

Mass Grave: Instant cast at 50+ Runic Power. Serves as a clutch, instant-cast AoE fear to turn the tide when swarmed.

The "Entomb" Execute Combo: Entomb an enemy at ~20% health, then instantly follow up with Death's Due. While niche, it provides a flashy execute option.
