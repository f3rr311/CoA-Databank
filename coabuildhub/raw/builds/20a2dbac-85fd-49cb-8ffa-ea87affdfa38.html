📊

Stats

Agility>Stamina>AP>Hit

Agility is the primary stat and provides significantly more value than attack power because many abilities and features scale with it. Gear similarly to a hunter. Spell power offers limited benefit, but it should not be prioritized.

Leveling

Levels 1–9 are slow and fairly standard, so pull one mob at a time.

From 10–20, Houndmaster is the strongest choice because the hound deals serious damage and serves as a temporary meat shield. It can level to 60, but efficiency drops after level 20, especially against elite quests where the hound dies too quickly.

Around level 21 is when i made the swap to Black Knight.
