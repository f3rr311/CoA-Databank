Overview

This build is mainly for random dungeon finder. Tome of Light provides an amazing uh oh button on a 5mins cd that lets your healer do dps for ~30seconds. Swap to Eternally devoted for most raid bosses or pushing mythic keys where you need 100% uptime on a specific Libram.

📊

Stats

(Hit/Exp) Agi > Stam > Dodge/parry > Def > Crit > Int> haste

(crit gives us damage reduction due to Sacred Oath Talent)

✨

Enchants

Focus on Hit/Exp then Stam and Agi
