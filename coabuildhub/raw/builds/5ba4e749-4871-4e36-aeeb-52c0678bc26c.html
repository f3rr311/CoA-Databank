Overview

Heretic Cultist let you heal your whole party by doing MASSIVE DAMAGE so if you always wanted to be a dps but you are stuck in the healing prison, this spec is made for you.

🌟

Talents

Lot of thing to say here. I'll start from bottom to top because the bottom rows are very important.

  • First, we take @Dark Prophet because it is the most broken aoe healing ability. It does around 25% to 40% of your overall healing in dungeon.
  • Next we have a choice between Hand of Yogg-Saron or @Void Barrier. It really depends on your group composition. If you think you are going to need an extra defensive spell for your tank, take Hand of Yogg-Saron, otherwise, you can take @Void Barrier for the extra 100% buff on your Void Shield .
  • 3rd row, we are getting buffs to our healing ability and also getting @Dark Chants which makes Black Blood stacks an additionnal time during his duration and a reduce cast time of your aoe shield Forbidden Ritual.
  • Dark Veil gives us an emergency button for when you are out of everything and you are unable to heal someone. It does around 8k shield on someone for 5 sec which gives enough time to recover cooldowns. @Saronite Cube is good for @Void Shield usage.
  • Finally, we don't have much to talk about, the rest of the talent are buffs to healing spells, we get @As The Prophecy Foretold for mana sustain, Eldritch Eye which makes the next 4 instance (Thanks to @Saronite Blessing) of either Malevolence or Eldritch Mending instant cast.

We do get @Abyssal Covenant which is a key talent for our healing. It allows us to convert our damage into heal to the guy your applied the buff on usualy the tank and also activate our biggest healing ability -> @Dark Prophet. Lastly, you will get a free instant Eldritch Mending every 3rd melee ability you cast, that can helps you heal your party without spending a CD on them.

⚔️

Rotation

  • Presence choice : You can either play with Presence of N'Zoth , Presence of C'Thun or Presence of Y'Shaarj.
  • Presence of N'Zoth to get the extra Tentacles healing thanks to Twisted Sanity but it won't be much. It does around 2% to 4% of your overall healing but atleast you get big Tentacles on your back.
  • Presence of C'Thun to get a nice and comfy 20% haste buff.
  • Presence of Y'Shaarj to get that extra overall damage on big pulls.
  • For the buffs, you can run Whispers of N'Zoth of the 12% total stats. If you play with a Sun Cleric, you can use Whispers of C'Thun for the SP since they apply the 12% total stats themself.

Now for the fight rotation, it's pretty simple. You keep Black Blood buff on your party all the time and just press your damage ability. Prioritizing Blade of the Empire since it's your main damage/healing ability, spend your sanity on Entropic Slam in 3+ monsters fight or Hammer of Twilight in singletarget/boss fight.

  • Malevolence: This is an important spell because it applies to 5 people Black Blood which is your number 1 spell on your list to keep track on. Note that the Hot duration doesn't refresh if you repress the spell. So you usually press it twice to stack the hot and repress it when the hot is gonna fade ~1 sec before so you don't lose time.
  • Herald of the Depths: Our only damage/healing burst spell. It increase your damage by 15%, which is nice, but the main part is it buff by 100% our absorption duration and healing over time effects. You get double value on your Black Blood duration.
  • During heavy fight where your party is going to receive a lot of damage, you can pre-stack your sanity to 80 with Satiate or by keeping your sanity high with the previous fight so you can press early your Herald of the Depths.

There's multiple way to heal during this kind of fight. You either spam damage or since we are in Herald form, you can apply massive shield to your whole party with Forbidden Ritual + Void Shield. This way gives your party around 3k to 5k shield which make the fight a lot easier in just 5 sec of applying shields.

  • Tentacles ? Yes Tentacles. I usually play around Tentacle of C'Thun for the damage and extra healing with Twisted Sanity but Tentacle of Yogg-Saron is nice to press in heavy damage fight where you have your Herald up and 2 stack of Black Blood, the 6% increased healing feels nice.
  • Gaze of C'Thun: I use this spell on CD kinda except when I know there's big damage incomming and I want a healing burst in that window thanks to Void-Touched passive talent which is massive healing.
  • Finally, for your mana and sanity usage, early in the game, where you are either not 60 and currently leveling or even when you are 60 and don't have that much gear/crit chance, you are going to use Sanity Tap a lot. Usually after every 3 to 4 gcd. It gives your 20% of your missing mana. Try to fit some Tap during your gameplay to not end up oom mid pull and let your party die. It requieres 20 sanity to press it ->Blade of the Empire gives your 20 Sanity and Malevolence 10.
📊

Stats

  • After some testing, Crit > Str/AP > SP/Int > Haste feels the best and fun. You don't really need that much mana if you have enough crit rate for your auto to regen mana with the talent As The Prophecy Foretold
  • With the stats choice, we can easily wear plates gear, which makes us tankier than running cloth/leather gear to get Int/SP.
  • You want to get a big 2H weapon, as slow as possible and either lot of Attack Power on it or Critical Strike + Strenght to get max value of our class.
