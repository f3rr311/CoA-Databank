Overview

Congratz, you are now a Furry on their period with anger issues :)

This build is primarily for people who like to spam dungeons while leveling

This is a WIP!

I am open for discussions, testing and theory-crafting stuff.

Warning

This class scales with Player Skill.

If you are confident with kiting, spacing, preemptive CD usage and on-the-spot adaptation you will excel!

If you pull pack-by-pack, you will be fine.

If you try to chain-pull without using the classes abilities and strengths you will be A) cosplaying as a furry floor-mat or B) your healers HPS will mean Heart-attacks per Second...

Post feedback/ideas/tips/secrets and i will work them into the guide.

GL & HF!

Talents

We are pretty locked in for the majority of the Talent Tree.

The Major debate is the choice for the last 2 Points.

Eternal Tree

Mentioning talents not picked:

Appetite for Blood: BIG DPS boost for your primary spam button. maybe worth dropping Rejuvenating Shadows for this.

Packleader: Currently bait, don't. Check if bat's remain after they hit once, if they stick around -> this may be worth.

Festering Maw: Can be good, but i would not bother with it since we are already very mobile.

Infection: Too slow for us. If we take a swing-timer of 3,3s this would take 33s to fully stack, and it has a 6 SECOND DURATION. It will fall off on any transition, any movement. You auto attack another enemy -> falls off. You miss one auto attack -> falls off. You don't have a bleed up -> falls off..... If you pick Infection your swing timer needs to be below 2.85s or you grief yourself!

Blood Moon/Shadows in the Night: it's meh at best. 3% heal of all damage is solid enough to justify picking it up, but the conditions cuck you too much. If they reverse conditions it may be worth.

Pick any Call of the Darkwing talents last, as their value comes from the finisher Shadow Strength.

For the finishers:

Bloody Claws: This talent is really good, for a "Blood Pact Chaining" Main Tank build, this is not a MT build :)

Eyes In The Night: As of right now, this is one of the weakest finishers i have seen. 1 Call of the Darkwing per 5 sec is just bad. And that is assuming full proc rate.

The threat is not worth mentioning, since who procs it is mostly random melees doing melee stuff, and at best it's a taunt on a 5s CD.

Class Tree

Change Blood Trails to Everhungry if you want a more DPS(ego) play-style.

With that logic you can change Blood Bond to Nightmare, Nightmare has some funny stuff going on, but it's mostly a DPS boost.

As for Transfusion, it's a good spell, but the alternatives are just better.

And Predator is never up for discussion, 1% Crit damage is not worth.

On the "Why do you pick Liquify and Bloodmoon Power over XYZ?" question:

  1. You can make a macro to cancelaura Curse Form and cast Liquify, thus we bypass the "requires mortal form".
  2. You can use it to buffer void zones or survive big "near boss" blasts or certain One Shot abilities/mechanics.
  3. The other Talents are useless or bait. The other options, if you don't wish to bother with Liquify, are Easy Prey and Life Scent.

TIP: Make your macro reusable, e.g. Cancel aura Curse Form -> Cast Liquify -> Cancle aura Liquify -> Cast Curse Form

If you belong in the "hit button 50 times" category, make 2 macro's. One to swap into Liquify, one to swap into Curse Form...

You do not want to get stuck in Liquify because the boss will be upset and decided he is gonna play with the melees if he can't diddle you! You essentially Blessing Of Protection yourself, you can work in the AOE Taunt into the macro if you want to.

(I will post macro's in a bit, still WIP segment)

For me Blood Bond is a must though, and the target is simple -> HEALER

There are plenty of caster mobs that stay stuck in nirvana and your healer is the only one stacking aggro on them, they CAN and WILL kill your healer, Blood Bond prevents that.

You can put Blood Bond on someone else, depending on either your dungeon knowledge or if you have a DPS buddy to coordinate with. Overall Blood Moon is a solid to VERY STRONG talent.

I do prefer to put it on the Healer for the simple reason that it gives Bro-Points, and that's always helpful.

Rotation

This Spec has more of a priority-list then a true rotation.

Group pulls

  1. Keep up Rot Claw, never let it sit on 2 stacks.
  2. Use Claw Sweep when you have Rage
  3. Use Rage generators, either the CD(HP -> 50 Rage converter) one, or the single target basic one.

###Single Target

  1. Keep up Hypovolemic Shock (AP debuff), Rotclaw, Bloodfang Bite
  2. Spam Bloodfang Bite
  3. Spam rage generator

As for How To dungeon pull:

  1. Tell your DPS's to watch their single target threat during grouping pulls!
  2. Use Rotclaw on the first and second pack you pull to lock them onto you. (DPS's will focus on these mobs most).
  3. Use Claw Sweep, or Rotclaw if available, when collecting packs along the way.
  4. Taunt and Hypovolimic shock to bring in stragglers.
  5. Once you have enough mobs start kiting in a Circle to group up the mobs, use your AOE while staying out of melee range.
  6. When the Mobs are starting to group up use your 30% increased HP CDs before going in!
  7. Use Blood Pact to face tank
  8. If it looks dicey -> start kiting again

How big you pull mostly depends on A) your skill and B) how based your healer is ¯\_(ツ)_/¯
