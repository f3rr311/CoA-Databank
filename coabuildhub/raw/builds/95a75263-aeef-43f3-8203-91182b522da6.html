Overview

🎵 🔥🔥 Pyromania - Cascada 🔥🔥 🎵

Flameweaving transforms our destructive fire magic into a force of restoration. While its core toolkit excels at sustained single-target healing, the specialization truly shines through its versatile and engaging resource management. As we heal and deal damage, we generate Embers - a secondary resource that fuels a variety of powerful and flexible abilities. Whether we need burst healing, team shielding, or extra damage, smart Ember management gives us the flexibility to adapt to every encounter.

You'll love the Flameweaving Pyromancer if you enjoy:

  • FIRE EVERYWHERE
  • Having a Phoenix pet
  • Nearly infinite mana
  • Secondary resource management
  • Weaving damage into your healing rotation
⚔️

Rotation

Mana

You'll rarely need to worry about mana after level 30 thanks to a combination of different passives and buffs.

  • Ashen Priest maintains 20% of your mana regeneration while in combat while also granting Replenishment whenever you critically heal, restoring 5% mana to yourself and your party.
  • Ashen Skin increases your in-combat mana regeneration by an additional 60%.
  • Shazzrah's Boon restores 2% of your total mana whenever you spend an Ember, providing excellent passive sustain alongside Seal of Al'ar, a buff we can apply to ourselves and others which restores mana every 5 seconds.

If you or a teammate ever begins running low on mana, Essence of Malygos restores 36% of total mana over 9 seconds.

Core Abilities & Gameplay

Flameweaving isn't built around a rigid rotation. Instead, success comes from understanding your toolkit and maintaining enough Embers to flex into whatever the situation requires.

Nearly every healing or damage spell that doesn't consume an Ember generates Heat.

100 Heat = 1 Ember

Throughout this guide, abilities that consume an Ember are marked with 🔥.

Mana Only

Phoenix Pet - Phoenix Egg

Your Phoenix should be active at all times. It lasts 60 seconds and pulses light AoE healing every 5 seconds (3 seconds with this build). Beyond its healing, several of your abilities require your Phoenix to be active.

See the Tips section for the WeakAura I use to track its duration and prevent it from expiring during important moments.

Emergency Cooldown(2 Mins) - Kael's Command

Requires your Phoenix to be active. For 10 seconds, your Phoenix pulses powerful AoE healing, making this your strongest answer when multiple party members begin dropping quickly. Don't be afraid to use it proactively during heavy group-wide damage.

Fast Heal - Kindle

A quick, medium-strength heal with no Ember cost.

Slow Heal - Cinderheart

A slower but significantly larger heal with no Ember cost.

Shield - Inferno Barrier

An instant single-target shield with a short cooldown. Best applied to the tank between pulls rather than during heavy combat.

AoE Damage / Healing - Cleansing Flames

Our bread and butter. This spell does an incredible amount of heavy lifting by dealing moderate damage while simultaneously healing and shielding allies in a cone.

Don't let the small visual effect fool you - the cone is far more generous than it appears. Position yourself to hit enemies while simultaneously healing and shielding your tank and nearby allies.

Large AoE Damage - Meteor

An instant-cast AoE with a cooldown. Drop this into large enemy packs before beginning your Cleansing Flames spam.

Ember Generator - Lava Shard

An instant-cast ranged nuke with a 10 second cooldown that immediately generates 1 Ember.

Use this on cooldown whenever you aren't Ember capped. If you're already sitting at maximum Embers, hold it until you've spent one to avoid wasting generation.

See the Tips section for a basic WeakAura cooldown tracker if you'd like one.

🔥 Ember Spenders

High Priority

🔥 Instant Heal - Ember Touch

An instant medium-strength heal with no cooldown. Excellent for saving fragile party members when there's no time to cast Kindle, or whenever you have Embers to spare. Use this liberally.

🔥 Group Shield - Phoenix Dive

An instant AoE shield with no cooldown.

Use this frequently between pulls or whenever you have excess Embers to cushion incoming damage before it happens. It shields allies in a medium-sized area around your target and requires your Phoenix to be active.

Lower Priority

🔥 Single Target Damage - Explode

Decent nuke if you have excess embers and literally nothing else to do.

🔥 Damage over Time - Blaze

Keep this on bosses if healing is under control and you have spare Embers.

🔥 Line Damage / Healing - Ignis Ultimatus

My class talent capstone of choice. After a short cast, unleash a massive line of fire that deals heavy damage to enemies while simultaneously providing moderate healing to allies caught in its path.

While it's generally not worth spending an Ember on cooldown, it becomes an excellent choice whenever your positioning allows you to hit several enemies while also healing multiple allies. Think of it as a more powerful version of Cleansing Flames that looks badass.

🔥 Multi-Target Damage - Flames of Neltharion

Hits your target and two nearby enemies.

In practice, it's usually better to be casting Cleansing Flames or almost anything else, making it a very low-priority Ember spender.

Typical Pull Strategy

Before each pull:

  • 🔥 Apply Phoenix Dive to shield the group before encounters.
  • Refresh Phoenix Egg if necessary.
  • Apply Inferno Barrier to the tank if you can spare the global cooldown.

Once combat begins:

  • Open with Meteor against large packs.
  • Position yourself so Cleansing Flames strikes enemies while covering the tank and as many party members as possible.
  • In easier content, Cleansing Flames alone will often handle the majority of your healing.
  • When heavy group damage is incoming, activate Kael's Command to stabilize the party.
  • As incoming damage increases, spend Embers aggressively on 🔥 Ember Touch and 🔥 Phoenix Dive to stabilize the group.

Stats

Spirit + Crit are your core priorities. Anything else is gravy.

Critical heals give us many synergies like mana regeneration and free shields.
