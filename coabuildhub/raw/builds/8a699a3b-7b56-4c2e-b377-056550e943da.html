Damage Rotation

Once you've completed the opener, your priority is to maintain your poisons while spending your combo points as efficiently as possible.

Priority

Keep Rootfang active at all times.

Generate combo points with Venom Fang.

Spend 5 combo points on Widowmaker whenever Rootfang has more than 5–6 seconds remaining.

If Rootfang has less than 5–6 seconds remaining, refresh it instead of using Rootfang.

Refresh Withering Venom and Nerubian Sting only when they are about to expire to avoid losing their stacks.

The rotation is simple: build combo points with Venom Fang, keep Rootfang active, and use Widowmaker as your main combo point spender whenever you have enough time left on Rootfang. Avoid refreshing your poison DoTs too early, as maintaining their stacks is a significant part of your sustained damage.

PvP

PvP Opener

This is the opener I use in most PvP situations. The goal is to apply all of your poisons as quickly as possible, build 5 combo points, and set up a powerful re-stealth for the kill.

Opener:

Brood Trap

Venom Fang from Skulk

Withering Venom

Nerubian Sting ×3

At this point, if I already have 5 combo points, I immediately use Rootfang (which is macroed with Contagiation).

If I'm still below 5 combo points, I cast one more Venom Fang to reach 5 combo points before using Rootfang.

During the opener, it's often a good idea to fit in a stun to prevent your target from reacting. Against casters, however, an interrupt is usually enough, so you can save your stun for later.

Once all of my poisons are applied and empowered by Contagiation through the Rootfang macro, I look for an opportunity to reset into stealth. Re-entering the fight from stealth while the target is already heavily pressured by multiple empowered poisons usually allows me to finish them off very quickly.

Macros

Useful Macros

These are the macros I use the most while playing Venomancer in PvP. They make the spec much smoother to play and help you react faster in key situations.

Rootfang + Contagiation

This macro casts Contagiation together with Rootfang. The macro uses the Rootfang icon.

#showtooltip Rootfang

/cast Rootfang

/cast Contagiation

----Spider Form + Skulk

This macro combines Spider Form with Skulk. You need to press it twice quickly: the first press shifts you into Spider Form, and the second activates Skulk. The macro uses the Skulk icon.

#showtooltip Skulk

/cast Spider Form

/cast Skulk

----Noxious Empowerment + Damage Trinket

This macro activates Noxious Empowerment and uses your upper trinket slot (13) at the same time.

#showtooltip Noxious Empowerment

/cast Noxious Empowerment

/cast Contagiation

/cast Use 13

----Defensive Macro (Lifeblood + Beetle Form)

This macro uses Lifeblood and immediately shifts you into Beetle Form for extra survivability.

#showtooltip Lifeblood

/cast Lifeblood

/cast Beetle Form
