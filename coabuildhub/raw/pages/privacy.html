Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Cookies

CoA Build Hub uses cookies to keep you signed in and to remember your preferences. Some cookies are set by the site itself and others are set by the third-party services described below.

Third-party advertising

This site is supported by advertising served through Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. In particular, Google's use of the DoubleClick DART cookie enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your prior visits to this site and other sites across the internet.

Opt-out

You can control or opt out of personalized advertising at any time:

  • Google Ads Settings
  • Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out
  • Google advertising policies

Account & first-party data

Accounts are handled through Supabase authentication, which supports signing in with Discord, Google, or email. When you sign in we store profile data such as your display name and avatar, along with the content you create — builds, votes, comments, collections, and follows.

Analytics

We use Vercel Analytics and Google Analytics (GA4) to understand aggregate site usage. Vercel Analytics is privacy-friendly and does not track you across other sites. Google Analytics sets cookies to measure how visitors find and use the site; you can learn how Google uses this data in Google's privacy & terms.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via our feedback page or on Discord.
